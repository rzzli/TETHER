# Freeze endpoint readiness before clinical prediction

The next deliverable is a **new, non-dispatch endpoint-readiness contract**, not another clinical run: define `reported_prespecified_efficacy_decision_v1`, its evidence schema, a finite origin-only source audit, and four separate release gates. The companion [v0.1 protocol](../planning/protocols/prospective-endpoint-readiness-v0.1.json) supplies those definitions while leaving requests, acquisition and clinical activation disabled. B0 remains stopped after 17/32 qualified January observations failed its 90% workload threshold; the completed harder-T6 development milestone does not supply clinical training labels or therapeutic truth. A proposed public-registry audit can test whether authentic origin-available decision-rule packages exist, but twelve records cannot size the clinical comparison. Qualified earlier training/calibration outcomes, a clinical scorer, an attainable reference route and a fully specified statistical plan remain launch prerequisites. Future test outcomes are required for later analysis, **not before freezing prospective predictions**.

## A documentary endpoint preserves the clinical question

The proposed unit is trial identifier × experimental product/regimen × indication/population × origin phase × named primary efficacy objective and designated analysis. The forecast concerns the reported disposition of that prespecified decision; it does not estimate causal benefit or assign efficacy to every target in a combination. Preserve the trial estimand's treatment/comparator, population, variable, intercurrent-event handling and population summary, plus analysis set, timing, threshold, multiplicity and interim rules. ICH defines those estimand attributes; this benchmark's operationalization is a **project choice**, not regulatory qualification. ([ICH E9(R1)](https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/E9-R1_Step4_Guideline_2019_1203.pdf))

Keep origin risk, decision occurrence, polarity, competing events, source qualification and temporal qualification separate. Success requires the authentic designated rule to be met; failure requires explicit non-attainment at the qualifying definitive analysis. Non-significance establishes failure of that rule, not absence of benefit. No report, inaccessible documents, missing rules, uncertain dates and unresolved contradictions yield null efficacy labels. A commercial/safety stop is a competing event only when evidence shows it prevented the designated decision; termination alone is insufficient. Positive evidence of no decision by horizon supports an occurrence diagnostic zero, never an efficacy negative.

Freeze the authentic whole co-primary objective, including its AND/OR logic and multiplicity; secondary/subgroup significance or interim continuation cannot substitute. FDA distinguishes all-required co-primary endpoints from success-on-any-endpoint families requiring multiplicity control. ([FDA multiple-endpoints guidance](https://www.fda.gov/media/162416/download)) Require the material success rule to exist and be available by future prediction origin. Later SAP clarification can be retained if consistent; material post-origin amendment cannot redefine the frozen endpoint. Preserve original-rule assessment or unknown/changed status, and report amended-rule results separately. ICH permits SAP finalization before unblinding after protocol finalization; this origin-fixed rule is deliberately a stricter project design choice. ([ICH E9, §5.1](https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/E9_Guideline.pdf))

## Twelve records test a route within a complete frame

The proposed acquisition population is a **complete closed 30-calendar-day first-public-posting interval**, ending before a later readiness capture date R. Dates remain null until separately sealed. Enumerate every record in that interval, then screen interventional, treatment-purpose, phase-2 or combined phase-2/phase-3 studies with a drug/biological intervention, across countries, indications and sponsors. Phase-1/phase-2 is excluded; missing/ambiguous required fields remain screen-unknown. Do not filter for documents, readouts, results availability or success. API metadata supports current records and explicit fields/pagination; successful schema/version requests establish neither study-endpoint transport nor complete document coverage. ([ClinicalTrials.gov OpenAPI](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/oas/v2), [field metadata](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/metadata))

Proposed operational caps are 500 interval records, 100 records/page, ten page attempts, 2 MiB/page, 20 MiB total and ten minutes, with one attempt per exact request. Exceeding any bound or failing complete pagination stops the frame: no first-500 truncation, moving interval or automatic retry. Reconcile unique identities, countTotal, terminal token and version checks; unchanged timestamps do not prove snapshot isolation. After frame sealing, exclude previously exposed identities under the frozen exposure policy, retain every disposition, and order eligible plus screen-unknown members by SHA256(protocol digest + canonical NCT), breaking ties by NCT. Select at most twelve without replacement, regardless of document availability; fewer candidates means a smaller audit. **Twelve is a workload choice, not power or population precision.**

Per selected record, allow one design/document listing and at most two exact protocol/SAP PDFs: 16 MiB each, 40-second request timeout, 120-second elapsed request ceiling, 250 MiB aggregate and thirty minutes aggregate. Preserve original bytes, identity/version bridges, source locators, receipts and hashes. Metadata flags, PRS upload date and document cover date do not independently prove an authentic rule or public availability at origin. A future capture before origin supplies observed availability; it cannot retrospectively reconstruct earlier access. Default access is public, unauthenticated and provider spending $0; no new user archive requirement has been established. AACT's available architecture does not repair this endpoint-evidence gap. ([AACT overview](https://aact.ctti-clinicaltrials.org/landing))

The current exact-NCT transport rejects query strings; existing document controls require a particular combined protocol/SAP product. Reuse receipts/hashes/sealing patterns only through a separately reviewed new contract. Leaf-field names/paths are frozen against the archived metadata in the [source check](../planning/review-assets/prospective-endpoint-readiness-20261023/metadata-source-check.json). Exact date-filter/query bytes and response allowlist remain **null with explicit blockers**, not a runnable manifest; they require schema review and offline guard tests before dispatch. Missing-post-date records outside this query-defined frame cannot be claimed enumerated, and mutable latest records do not form a historical snapshot. Results, results dates, stop reasons, outcome-bearing status and publications are prohibited selection inputs; accidental outcome text is quarantined, exposure recorded and never used for replacements.

## Blinded source extraction can qualify documentary references

Two isolated agents can independently extract the same source-only packet, seal outputs, and pass discrepancies to a separate reconciler blinded to predictions and comparator identities. Each mandatory field needs original-source evidence, exact locator, version and supported time interval; reconciliation checks those originals rather than voting. Shared model/source biases remain limitations. Agreement measures reproducibility and cannot manufacture clinical truth. No human-only gate is introduced for this objective documentary task; original H5 expert preference remains a different hypothesis requiring its specified assessment or an explicit amendment.

Capture rule existence by origin, prespecification before relevant unblinding/analysis, public availability and decision timing independently. A disclosure supplies an upper bound on decision time, not an exact date. In-window classification requires the entire supported decision interval inside `(F,H]`; boundary-straddling dates stay indeterminate. Freeze a genuinely future origin F only after launch gates pass. Twelve months is a candidate occurrence horizon; the separate reporting allowance remains unresolved and must be justified before launch. Late disclosure cannot silently extend the primary ascertainment deadline. Public reporting can depend on result direction, so missingness cannot be assumed independent. ([Stern and Simes](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9310565/))

## Four gates prevent readiness from becoming activation

**Design acceptance** checks the schema, choices, caps, provenance, exposure and blockers; it authorizes zero queries, members, labels or predictions. **Readiness acquisition** additionally requires sealed dates, exact schema-reviewed request bytes, safe transport/new-product contracts, source separation and a sealed executable request satisfying the existing authorized scope. Zero integrity/boundary violations and all-member dispositions are mandatory. At least one complete joint-ready package demonstrates route existence only; zero packages stops that route even when partial packages exist. Readiness is assessed at R; origin risk and the unchanged material rule must be requalified at actual future F. Partial/conflicted packages require a named revision before further acquisition, without replacing failures. No B0-derived 90% clinical threshold is invented.

**Prospective prediction launch** separately requires endpoint-qualified earlier training/calibration outcomes, origin-eligible features, component-separated leakage controls, qualified fitted/calibrated scorer, matched comparator recipe, representative evaluation design, feasible reference route, justified event/precision assumptions and a frozen analysis plan. Preserve probabilities null and numerical features empty until qualification. Size the actual paired analysis under dependence, missingness, label error and abstention; record counts cannot establish information. Validation sample size depends on intended precision and metric, with trial-program application requiring additional assumptions. ([Pavlou et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8529102/)) Original one-sided H1 conflicts with the retained proxy's two-sided statistic; resolve this before outputs. The co-primary Brier success criterion and H1/H2 multiplicity are also unresolved. Proposed 80% power at AUROC gain 0.05 is a project target, not an attained result. Freeze models, ancestry, prompts, selection, abstention and predictions before follow-up; immutable pre-origin weights do not prove an exact training cutoff.

**Later analysis release** requires realized qualified decisions, predetermined ascertainment, paired scores and sufficient observed information under the frozen analysis. Preserve competing events, unknowns and all-member denominators; conditional decision-complete performance is explicitly conditional. Freeze product/trial/regimen dependency components and sponsor/target sensitivities; indication-only bootstrap cannot silently ignore cross-indication dependence. Whole-frame binary claims require an explicit missingness/competing-event estimand. Original Tasks 6–8 remain open: H3 needs dated popularity adjustment; H4 qualified genetics; H5 claim-appropriate independent references; T2 qualified matched outcomes; T3 a candidate universe and positive-unlabeled treatment; T4 action/modality/tissue truth; T5 qualified safety stops; H6 its original analysis matrix and covariates.

Root continues safe technical work autonomously; only established file, credential or permission problems require user action. A successful route test supports designing a sized study, not automatic clinical activation. Preserve old dates, failed studies, historical exclusions and exposed development cases; this document changes neither their labels nor their permissions.
