# TETHER Needs Matched Evidence Quality Tests

**Use an independent conformance comparison to test TETHER's distinctive assurance contract, then SciFact and one biomedical QA task to test whether that assurance helps produce useful, supported answers.** No inspected external benchmark jointly measures information-availability cutoffs, hypothesis scope and transitive lineage. SciFact is the best first semantic extension; ALCE separates citation support from answer correctness; LAB-Bench's literature/database questions offer the most proportionate initial biomedical utility test. None is ready for native execution through the current qualified offline extractor. Several valuable peer methods are absent or only partly qualified, but fixed failure denominators, paired policy projections, declared dependence grouping and basic caching already exist. The important next contribution is a measurable improvement at matched utility and cost, not another perfect source-copying score or more agent roles. This report proposes evaluation and method transfers; it reports no new benchmark result. ## Benchmark fit depends on the claim being tested

TETHER needs two distinct endpoints. **Assurance performance** concerns admissibility, source identity, reproducible derivation, accounting and recovery. **Answer performance** concerns correct interpretation or useful decisions. An answer can be scientifically correct while using ineligible evidence; a fully traceable answer can misinterpret its source. A biomedical QA score mainly evaluates an attached solver unless the design isolates the wrapper's intervention. Conversely, checking supplied gold answers tests transport and verification, not prediction. This separation is consistent with ALCE's distinction between answer correctness and citation quality, and with SciFact's evidence-plus-label evaluation. ([ALCE](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/); [SciFact](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.609/))

The recommended portfolio is deliberately small. Start with the proposed conformance suite, then **SciFact plus one of LitQA2 or DbQA** if a task-capable component is developed. ALCE is a strong additional citation test when free-text generation becomes relevant. The remaining options answer narrower or later questions rather than supplying interchangeable headline scores.

SciFact's rationale rules prevent a permissive quote-overlap shortcut: correct source selection, judgment and sufficient rationale must agree. Existing public development labels avoid new annotation recruitment, but they are not a blind leaderboard test. Likewise, using ALCE's fixed passages is useful only if the arm is disclosed and gold answers remain isolated. **The current $0/offline scope permits preparing contracts and replaying available candidate outputs, not claiming original benchmark competence without a solver.** No model/evaluator weights, complete storage requirements or native scorer execution were qualified here. ([SciFact schema](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/allenai/scifact/master/doc/data.md); [ALCE instructions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/princeton-nlp/ALCE/main/README.md))

Several tempting alternatives should wait. R2E's public Gene Description Facts and held-out literature support candidate ranking with MRR/hits metrics, but TETHER lacks a qualified ranker and candidate universe. Its separately released clinical labels do not repair the project's chronology, outcome and prediction prerequisites. BixBench v1.5 offers computational-biology tasks and trajectory replay; that requires sandboxed Python/R/Bash and an analytical producer. The current card lists 205 questions, while repository capsule counts vary by release: freeze and enumerate the selected version. AgentDojo is valuable once dynamic actions exist; a no-tool verifier cannot win an interactive security benchmark by exposing nothing to attack. ([R2E release](https://github.com/BenevolentAI/r2e-evaluation-data); [BixBench card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/futurehouse/BixBench); [runner](https://github.com/Future-House/BixBench); [AgentDojo](https://github.com/ethz-spylab/agentdojo))

Availability also narrows choices. BiomniBench-DA exposes a gated **50 of 100 tasks**, reports 82.9 GB and uses a Gemini judge; public-half performance cannot represent the full benchmark. TxBench-PP's public repository exposes **seven examples** from the paper's 100 evaluations. OriGene TRQA is Target Retrieval QA: its three CSVs have different schemas and the inspected Hugging Face release reports a cast error; clinical-stage recall is not efficacy forecasting. Insilico TargetBench concerns retrospective target retrieval/association and acknowledges leakage limitations. These are useful later references, not shortcuts to clinical validation. ([BiomniBench card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/phylobio/BiomniBench-DA); [TxBench release](https://github.com/latchbio/txbench-pp); [TRQA files](https://github.com/GENTEL-lab/OriGene/tree/main/TRQA_benchmark); [TRQA card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/GENTEL-Lab/TRQA); [TargetBench article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-47765-3))

## Peer methods reveal real gaps and existing strengths

The largest gap is **semantic support independent of provenance**. The current interpretation codec checks identities, exact premises and structure but explicitly excludes entailment. The T6 audit utility accepts supplied support/contradiction labels and checks cited bytes; it does not establish those labels' truth. Adding a calibrated semantic component would be a substantive extension, whereas renaming that utility as a verifier would not. ([Codec](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/src/asof/study/diagnostic_codec.py:603>); [label-audit limit](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/tools/science/t6_claim_audit.py:186>))

The code already implements several methods often recommended to agent projects. Scheduled-cell aggregation rejects missing/duplicate results and retains failures. FULL/WITHOUT_WRAPPER/WIRE_ONLY use identical retained outputs, although these are policy projections rather than independently executed/timed systems. Dependency groups union declared study/cohort identities; unresolved identities remain a scientific data gap. There are bounded caches, stable-read controls, source replay and hash-chained attempt records. The correct recommendation is to extend and test these mechanisms, not claim they are absent. ([Aggregation](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/src/asof/study/source_fact_evaluation.py:192>); [paired projections](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/paper/revisions/20261102/manuscript.md:79>); [dependence grouping](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/src/asof/features.py:34>); [cache](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/src/asof/wire.py:35>); [journal](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/src/asof/journal.py:77>))

| Method to adopt or strengthen | Peer contribution and actual TETHER gap | Acceptance or falsification; priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Independent full-policy comparator** | Existing source-only auditing is distinct from a separately implemented full admission/accounting/recovery validator. This is an experimental-design recommendation, not a claim that a named peer already supplies the identical oracle. | Match policy, inputs, proposed outputs and budgets. Equal correctness with greater TETHER cost/complexity defeats an efficiency or architectural-superiority claim. **First**, after the P2 fixes; several engineering days, potentially longer if policy ambiguity emerges. |
| **Behavioral and metamorphic test matrix** | CheckList organizes minimum-functionality, invariant and directional behaviors. TETHER already has tamper/boundary fixtures; the missing evidence is a separately specified, frozen family matrix with valid neighbors, combined faults and genuinely held-out constructions. [CheckList](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.442/) | Reordering irrelevant records must preserve decisions; a late required ancestor must change eligibility; valid new computation over old inputs must remain allowed. A changed action need not imply contradiction. Measure both false acceptance and rejection. **First**; no new model/data needed. |
| **Semantic checking separated from source checking** | ALCE/ARES separate answer quality, relevance and faithfulness; Alvessa tests evidence-conditioned checking. TETHER's exact quotes and supplied labels do not provide an independently evaluated entailment decision. [ARES](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.20/); [Alvessa](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12773016/) | SciFact labels and official scorer test support/refutation/insufficiency; compare evidence versus no/irrelevant evidence, with every output retained. Reject benefit if the judge only agrees with its generator or if errors fall only by excessive rejection. **Next capability**; adapter plus qualified semantic model/evaluator, roughly one to several weeks. |
| **Utility/error/coverage and failure decomposition** | AgentDojo measures attack outcomes alongside legitimate utility; Inspect distinguishes incorrect outputs from evaluator failure. Current TETHER all-case accounting already supports part of this. [AgentDojo paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.13352v1); [Inspect policy](https://inspect.aisi.org.uk/scoring-policy.html) | Report correct-and-admitted answers per scheduled case, false admission/rejection, abstention and operational/judge failures. Compare semantic error at matched coverage as secondary analysis. Reject-all must have zero useful task success. **Immediate analysis design**; existing aggregate panels require hours, external validation more. |
| **Study-qualified evidence interventions** | R2E masks answer identity and attributes model scores to evidence sets. TETHER records coefficient contributions and declared dependence but lacks qualified evidence-set sensitivity for a useful score. [R2E](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.167/) | Once a ranker exists, remove/add independent study groups, compare random removals and duplicate evidence. A score change is model attribution, not a causal drug effect. **Later**; study/cohort metadata and a defined estimand are prerequisites. |
| **Safe completed-step and immutable-value reuse** | Nextflow makes task identity and successful outputs explicit. TETHER already caches some parsing, but the prior audit measured repeated task hashing/source parsing. [Nextflow](https://docs.seqera.io/nextflow/cache-and-resume); [file-cache modes](https://docs.seqera.io/nextflow/reference/process/directives/cache) | Preserve byte/decision equivalence under replacement, parent/policy change and interruption; then compare cold/warm time, memory and logical bytes. Do not substitute mtime keys for content assurance or replay unresolved calls. **After correctness**; several engineering days. |
| **Repeated reliability and calibrated abstention** | τ-bench's `pass^k` measures success across all k trials rather than any one success. ARES/SAFER require reference/calibration assumptions for statistical claims. Current deterministic coverage thresholds are not calibrated error risk. [τ-bench v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.12045v1); [SAFER](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/582e9771ac8527cb6390e5e9444a0fee-Abstract-Conference.html) | Prospectively schedule all trials, retain failures and calibrate separately. Identical deterministic replays do not establish stochastic reliability; unresolved biomedical dependence prevents automatic transfer of guarantees. **Later**, only under a new stochastic protocol. |
| **Tool-action capabilities and explicit enforcement events** | CaMeL separates trusted control from untrusted data; AgentSpec defines triggers/predicates/actions. TETHER's admitted-ID selection and artifact policy are narrower than general side-effect authorization. [CaMeL](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.18813v1); [AgentSpec 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.18666v1) | With equal tool affordances, measure benign task success, forbidden effects and false blocking under attacks. These designs do not establish text entailment or publisher truth. **Architectural extension later**; low value for the present no-action route. |

These are transfers with conditions, not forecasts of peer-sized gains. More agent roles, debate, self-evolving memory or repeated self-criticism do not solve the present endpoint's literal ceiling. They introduce cost and potential information leakage unless a new task demands them. Likewise, copying a benchmark's LLM judge does not produce independent scientific adjudication. Public existing annotations can support a bounded evaluation without new expert recruitment; novel biomedical truth remains unqualified when no suitable reference exists.

The citation relations are narrower than the conceptual comparison. SciFact's scoring guide explicitly relates its rationale cap to FEVER; AgentDojo directly contrasts/cites ToolEmu. The earlier reviewed TxBench references connect it to LAB-Bench, BixBench and BiomniBench, and R2E connects retrieval with attribution predecessors. CaMeL, Nextflow and CheckList are methodological analogies here, not claimed ancestors of TETHER. The inspected τ-bench README now calls its original tasks outdated, and its successor documents noncomparability between changed versions: borrow the reliability definition, not an unpinned leaderboard number. ([SciFact scoring](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/allenai/scifact/master/doc/evaluation.md); [AgentDojo](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.13352v1); [TxBench references](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.19245v2); [original τ-bench warning](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sierra-research/tau-bench/main/README.md); [successor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sierra-research/tau2-bench/main/README.md))

## A staged experiment can isolate useful assurance

The main question should be frozen before implementation: **does TETHER prevent more invalid use while retaining useful valid outputs, at an acceptable incremental cost, than a simpler implementation enforcing the same contract?** A weaker hash/schema-only baseline is a diagnostic ablation. Beating it identifies the value of omitted requirements, not the value of TETHER's architecture. Full Virtual Biotech reproduction is a separate system comparison with different resources and scientific endpoints, not the lean validator proposed here. ([Pinned Virtual Biotech implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harrisongzhang/TheVirtualBiotech/71f9da68c95c5ee0ef4c1d9b8f45f10693de022d/README.md))

Also test the architectural alternative: could these obligations be a small adapter/scorer extension within an existing evaluation runner such as Inspect? Its documented outcome handling is relevant infrastructure, not proof that it already enforces TETHER's lineage policy. An integration prototype should compare code/maintenance surface, interoperability, retained diagnostics and equal-policy correctness against the standalone path. This is a proposed engineering comparison, not an implemented integration or a claim that either design is inherently better. ([Inspect scoring policy](https://inspect.aisi.org.uk/scoring-policy.html))

| Stage and boundary | Concrete design and outputs | Gate before proceeding |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Correct and specify** | Fix CE01–CE03 in a newly qualified version; retain old artifacts. Write expected admission/accounting/recovery behavior independently of candidate code. Define valid cases, fault families, threat model and scientific/non-scientific claims. | Receipt aliasing, lost validated usage and unused-node temporal contradictions fail paired regressions. Those exposed witnesses remain development cases, not unseen tests. |
| **B. Conformance without a model** | Replay identical proposed outputs and source bytes through TETHER, an independent full-policy validator, hash/schema ablation and reject-all sanity control. Include valid near misses, combined faults, mutation/interruption and source-size/lineage-depth variation. | Independent expected outcomes; no privileged labels in producer inputs; complete terminal records. This is a **new TETHER-derived suite**, not an official external benchmark. |
| **C. One external semantic component** | Pin SciFact public development/evaluation route and licenses; separate tuning, calibration and held-out groups. Build an entailment/evidence adapter and run the official scorer alongside provenance/eligibility checks. | Qualified task-capable component, evaluator reproducibility and budget. Recorded-candidate replay alone remains checker evaluation; it cannot be relabeled end-to-end SciFact performance. |
| **D. One useful biomedical task** | Choose LitQA2 or DbQA by desired use, not favorable results. Freeze source/retrieval/answer affordances. Compare the same base producer with wrappers under matched inputs and budgets; retain native answer accuracy plus correct-and-admitted utility. | Source access, answer isolation, minimal valid serialization within budget, independent reference scoring and complete attempts. A small feasibility slice is not a powered final study. |
| **E. Broaden only after benefit appears** | Add ALCE free-text citation evaluation; conditional GenomeArena/RAGPPI/CRAG; later ranking, analysis or action security only if those capabilities exist. | Original protocols and derivatives remain separate. Stop expansion if equal-policy comparison shows no useful incremental benefit. |

For the verifier experiment, **false admission among known-invalid proposals and false rejection among known-valid proposals are co-primary**. In task evaluation, the primary utility denominator is every scheduled case; count correct-and-admitted outputs, not merely accuracy among accepted answers. Secondary measures include rationale/citation quality, source replay, coverage, abstention, failure attribution, recovery completeness, wall/CPU time, peak memory and logical bytes. Cost includes failed attempts, preparation, verification and independent audit. Where model usage is unknown, retain unknown rather than replacing it with a favorable estimate. Never combine semantic confidence and provenance eligibility into a single number that hides their different meanings.

Use paired outcome tables and task/source/fault-family summaries. A claim with many rationale sentences, several transformations of one seed, or multiple questions from one capsule does not create independent observations. Partition and resample at the justified task/source cluster; biological studies additionally require real study/cohort identities. Choose sample size after defining a useful effect and paired disagreement assumptions. A proposed 20–30-case feasibility slice tests adapters and costs, not statistical power. A later stochastic experiment can predeclare repeated trials and report all-k reliability; it must not rescue previously closed failures with selected retries. ([Current descriptive-only inference](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/src/asof/evaluation.py:118>); [current reporting denominator](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/src/asof/study/source_fact_evaluation.py:192>))

End-to-end wrappers can alter later actions, so identical final trajectories are not a fair requirement there. Instead fix the initial producer, model/version, source snapshot, prompt, tools, budgets and attacks; counterbalance order and retain realized behavior. Verifier-only replay holds outputs exactly fixed. This two-level design distinguishes checker effects from solver competence. Equal correctness with higher cost would narrow TETHER to a transparent reference implementation; fewer false admissions paired with unacceptable valid-answer loss would expose a utility failure. Both are legitimate outcomes, predeclared rather than explained away afterward.

## The strongest paper extension is narrower and more falsifiable

Three proposed result panels would materially strengthen the argument: **valid/invalid admission by independently specified fault family; semantic error versus retained-answer coverage on external labels; and cost versus source size/lineage depth at unchanged assurance**. Keep native benchmark answer scores beside those panels, not blended into a composite “trust” score. The project can prepare the conformance protocol and descriptive panels with existing resources. Scientific entailment and answering require explicit new components; release-matched study/cohort/variant/effect metadata would be useful only when a defined genetics or ranking endpoint justifies them. No additional user files or expert recruitment are needed to choose the first protocol.

This was a bounded seed-driven review, not an exhaustive survey or benchmark execution. Primary pages were checked on 2 November 2026; some full texts/raw cards failed and indexed primary excerpts or official rendered pages supplied the stated limited facts. Dataset integrity, full source coverage, licenses of every embedded asset, native scorer operation and expanded storage remain unqualified. Public viewers exposed some answer-bearing BixBench/TRQA/RAGPPI examples; the retained exposure ledger marks them as development exposure and omits their answers. They cannot become a pristine holdout by relocation, and unviewed public items are not guaranteed absent from model training. **Eligible retrieved inputs do not establish that a model's weights lack later information.** Any future historical claim must separately address model chronology and training exposure; a CRAG query time or R2E-style literature cutoff alone cannot establish that guarantee. ([Biomedical access/exposure ledger](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/research_notes/TETHER benchmarks and methodological advances/biomedical_benchmarks.md>); [general source/access ledger](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/research_notes/TETHER benchmarks and methodological advances/general_benchmarks_methods.md>))

The scientific opportunity is an honest test of incremental assurance across independently labeled content and explicitly controlled provenance errors. Success would justify expanding capabilities; failure would identify which safeguards or architectural layers are not earning their cost.
