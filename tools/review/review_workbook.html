Help us judge whether each recorded statement is supported by its source, relevant to the proposed intervention, and presented with enough scientific context.

How to review • definitions and scope
  1. Read the hypothesis and the two evidence cards. Review the proposed action and modality, not only the target.
  2. Open references as needed. Expand reported context to inspect model, variant and tissue information.
  3. Answer six short questions for each excerpt and explain your judgment. “Cannot assess” is a valid answer.
  4. Rate the usefulness of this excerpt, then move to the next case. You can stop and resume.
  5. Export your answers and send the file to the study coordinator. Export a backup before moving this file or switching browsers.

Severity: minor = presentation issue unlikely to change interpretation; major = error that could change the biological conclusion; critical = an error that could invalidate the evidence or reverse a decision. Use “No issue identified” only after assessment.

Scope: an unblinded review of 20 purposively selected source-audit records, not a complete dossier review, representative benchmark or clinical prediction. Shared targets and studies are not independent samples. Provider scores are not effect sizes or clinical probabilities.

Source dates: Open Targets 25.09; HGNC reference dated October 10, 2026. Each case retains its exact cutoff in provenance. External reference pages may show newer versions. The workbook contains normalized source records, not full articles.

Completion: all six choices plus a rationale complete one evidence review. All 20 reviews, ten excerpt ratings and a reviewer ID complete this review form. Scientific adjudication remains a separate step.

Review & export

Exports include unfinished answers as blanks and retain source identifiers. Send the downloaded answers file to the coordinator. A completed form is a single-reviewer assessment; it is not adjudicated gold.
