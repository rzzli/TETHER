## Read the current paper

1. Open the [18-page combined paper and supplement](paper/asof-source-reporting-preprint-v2.pdf).
2. Use the editable [main text](paper/manuscript.md),
   [supplement](paper/supplement.md), [bibliography](paper/references.bib),

4. The unfilled [author-declaration checklist](paper/AUTHOR-DECLARATIONS.md)
   records the remaining factual inputs. Unknown declarations have not become
   statements of no funding or no conflict. External expert responses are not a
   prerequisite or an input to this study.

This paper reports a bounded technical source-fidelity study: twelve selected
native units, fourteen exposed constructed controls, 38 outputs and 90 policy
decisions. It does not establish causal genetics, clinical prediction,
independent-study generalization, model superiority or expert equivalence.
Numerical prediction features remain empty and clinical probabilities null.
AI assistance and the closed negative local-model development branch are
disclosed in the paper.

Verify the whole packet ZIP against the separately retained external packet
hash record before relying on its internal manifest. A hash supplied only by an
untrusted archive is insufficient authority. No original source/control outputs,
reference answers, private journals, human-response descendants, protected
outcomes, credentials, unconfirmed owner facts, model weights or standalone font files
are included by the outer packet allowlist. The nested P09 rights exceptions
remain explicit. Technical byte checks do not establish publisher authenticity,
