## 1. What is implemented, and what results actually exist?

### Takeaway

TETHER is currently strongest as an evidence-admission, source-reporting and execution-verification harness with specialist selection and optional bounded local generation interfaces. It has engineering qualification and a small literal-reporting study; it has no executed native SciFact/LAB-Bench result, no completed equal-policy comparison and no Inspect implementation in the reviewed active tree. The retained empirical study is narrow: twelve source-selected native units, 180 requested facts, a literal assembler and always-abstain baseline. Literal reporting is exact in 12/12; abstention completes 0/12. The constructed policies admit 0/10, 2/10 and 7/10 invalid scenarios, with zero false rejections among four technically valid controls. These are **policy ablations on exposed constructed controls**, not complete peer systems or general scientific performance. [Native table](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/paper/results/tables/native_case_results.md:1), [policy table](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/paper/results/tables/constructed_policy_summary.md:1), [revised manuscript](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/paper/revisions/20261102/manuscript.md:77).

Production took 203.828 seconds, including 197.812 seconds source verification; logical completed hash reads total 140,452,303,784 bytes. Actual physical disk traffic and per-arm process-memory attribution are unknown. The later corrected manuscript explicitly states that 80% and 100% completion thresholds are identical for this native set. A previous five-call local-model development pilot produced zero accepted model reports and its two-call conformance follow-up closed the branch under a stop rule. These observations cannot support a broad model-inability claim. [Resources](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/paper/results/tables/resource_phases.md:1), [threshold correction](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/paper/revisions/20261102/manuscript.md:85), [negative pilot](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/paper/revisions/20261102/manuscript.md:139).

### Inferences

- A stronger paper should test **incremental useful completion under matched policy and candidate conditions**. More successful hashes or perfect copying of supplied values cannot establish an agent advantage because the task's ceiling is already reached by the literal assembler. This inference follows from the emitted-fact and constructed-policy results, not an assumption about competitors. [Results](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/paper/results/tables/native_case_results.md:1), [discussion](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/paper/revisions/20261102/manuscript.md:149).
- Generic benchmark task adapters should use a separate typed study contract/`StudyVerifier` rather than inventing therapeutic target IDs merely to fit `Case(task=T1/T6)`. The latter would obscure benchmark semantics; the former already provides a bounded integration seam. [Case restriction](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/src/asof/contracts.py:146), [study protocol](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/src/asof/study/session.py:87).

### Gaps

- BG02 freeze, scheduled paired execution, independent reconstruction and final interpretation remain undone. The unscored footprint classifier currently omits `independent_runtime.py` from the independent-validator set and classifies it as infrastructure; correct that accounting before a maintenance-cost claim. No source change was made in this research pass. [Classifier](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/experiments/equal_policy/reconstruct.py:354).
- The frozen study has unresolved prior exposure and source-study/cohort independence. A future counterfactual/benchmark study needs its own protocol; it cannot retrospectively turn these twelve units into a holdout. [Limitations](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/paper/revisions/20261102/manuscript.md:159).

## 2. Which peer-style improvements are genuinely missing versus already present?

The largest missing capability is evaluated **answer/evidence support**, followed by evidence-seeking retrieval and calibrated useful abstention. Provenance graphs, hypothesis binding, declared dependence grouping, fixed role chains and replay/accounting already exist; their incremental value and appropriate reuse require testing, rather than renaming them as new methods. ### Inferences

- Use two complementary comparisons: (a) fixed candidate artifacts to identify the causal effect of admission rules, and (b) fixed model, sources, tools and budgets to assess end-to-end workflow behavior. Retain model-only/ungated, independent equal-policy, TETHER and later Inspect-with-the-same-policy arms where they answer the question. A weaker hash-only arm is diagnostic and cannot establish architectural superiority. [Authorized matched-policy design](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/planning/TETHER Benchmark Execution Plan 2026-11-02.md:19>), [native task comparison](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/planning/TETHER Benchmark Execution Plan 2026-11-02.md:27>).
- Score **correct-and-admitted useful completions over all scheduled tasks** alongside unsafe acceptance, valid rejection, abstention, task/model/infrastructure failures and cost. Restricting accuracy to admitted outputs can make a reject-all policy look reliable; repeated seeds/decisions do not increase independent task sample size. [All-cell aggregator](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/src/asof/study/source_fact_evaluation.py:192), [contract coverage](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/planning/review-assets/tether-equal-policy-20261102/COVERAGE-QUALIFICATION-v2.md).
- Input chronology does not establish weight-training chronology. Identity/evidence interventions are worthwhile diagnostics, but changes under blinding are not by themselves proof of memorization because masking changes the task context. The repository itself records the weight-chronology limit; the proposed causal experiment needs matched controls. [Memory](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/memory/project-goals.md:24), [source exposure](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/paper/revisions/20261102/manuscript.md:63).

### Gaps

- “Absent” here is bounded to the pinned active-tree search plus inspected modules, not every archived script or external dependency. Entailment strings occur in review/limit code; the existence of that word is not a fitted semantic evaluator. The historical `fixed-evidence-plan-v1.json` contains prospective labels while later packaged study machinery exists, so its old status cannot describe the entire repository. [Search scope](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/research_notes/TETHER benchmark fit and advances/current_project_gap_search_scope.json>), [study implementation](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/src/asof/study/authority_four_arm.py:811).
- No claim about novelty or missing competitor capabilities follows from this local audit. The parent survey must check peer implementations and current official benchmark specifications separately; exact-once dispatch, typed agents and provenance should not be presented as uniquely TETHER methods. [Project's existing positioning limit](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/paper/revisions/20261102/manuscript.md:151).

### Inferences

- The strongest near-term scientific addition is an actual answer task where exact source copying is insufficient, coupled with valid-case retention. That gives the harness something meaningful to improve or harm; a negative result is still informative. [Current ceiling](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/paper/revisions/20261102/manuscript.md:149), [BG03/BG04 requirements](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/planning/TETHER Benchmark Execution Plan 2026-11-02.md:27>).
- Existing hardware/local-model access can support a bounded baseline subject to capacity/format qualification, but the previous negative branch must remain closed under its frozen protocol. New benchmark work requires a new explicit protocol rather than repairing old outcomes. [Closed model branch](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/paper/revisions/20261102/manuscript.md:141), [execution boundary](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/planning/TETHER Benchmark Execution Plan 2026-11-02.md:9>).
- Generic public benchmark labels are sufficient for native task scoring; recruiting new external experts is not a prerequisite for these stages. Owner-only needs arise if a selected source requires an account/license or new authorization, and deferred author declarations remain a separate publication issue. R2E, TRQA, GenomeArena, TargetBench, BiomniBench, TxBench and DrugAgent remain **planned collection** entries in the inspected catalog; a catalog entry is not a collected runnable benchmark. [Scientific limits](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/paper/revisions/20261102/manuscript.md:153), [unfinished programs](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/paper/revisions/20261102/manuscript.md:167), [catalog](D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/data/catalog/sources.json).
