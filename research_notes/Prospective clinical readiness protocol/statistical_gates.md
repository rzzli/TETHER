# Prospective clinical readiness: statistical and chronology activation contract

## What can readiness establish, and what must remain separate?

### Takeaway
Produce a finite, endpoint-specific **non-dispatch readiness protocol** now. Its acceptance establishes that the proposed feasibility procedure is specified and auditable; neither acceptance nor a twelve-record pilot establishes clinical label availability, statistical power, a trained scorer or activation of H1/H2.

### Cited Findings
- The October 23 disposition explicitly calls for this artifact before new trial extraction/search, preserves original H1–H6, and leaves clinical activation deferred. The B0 result is 17/32 below its frozen 90% workload threshold; the threshold is not a clinical rule. — [Remaining gates](../../planning/Remaining%20Scientific%20Gates%20and%20Prospective%20Design%202026-10-23.md).
- Original H1 compares clinical discrimination against Virtual Biotech, with paired AUROC and Brier co-primary; H2 compares against genetics-only and time-masked Open Targets. The proposed 0.05 AUROC gain is the smallest effect of interest, and the original table specifies a one-sided H1 test. “Better” requires both H1 and H2. — [Original proposal](../../planning/Beyond%20the%20Virtual%20Biotech%20Tooling%20Gaps%20and%20Project%20Plan.md).
- The retained next-twelve source-readiness protocol is inactive after retrieval calibration failure, keeps a fixed denominator with partial/unknown/inapplicable cases, and explicitly disallows population confidence intervals or power claims from its purposive exposed sample. — [Inactive protocol](../../planning/protocols/source-readiness-feasibility-v0.1.json).
- External-validation sample size depends on intended precision, outcome prevalence, anticipated discrimination and the metric being evaluated; a universal record count is unsupported. This methodological work concerns risk models, so application to trial/program prediction requires project-specific assumptions. — [Pavlou et al., original methods study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8529102/).

### Inferences
**Project judgments: three separate release decisions.**

1. **Readiness-design acceptance, available now:** all mandatory fields, source-frame definition, sample algorithm, caps, exposure register, measurement rules and review procedures are present; no unresolved contradictory permission. Acceptance has zero new members, zero queries, zero labels and zero predictions. Preserve `clinical_activation=false`, `probabilities=null`, an empty numeric feature set and explicit training prohibition.
2. **Readiness-pilot authorization, conditional later:** a separately frozen acquisition contract and source seals pass technical controls; origin-only membership is sealed before opening follow-up. This authorizes only bounded source/readiness measurement, not fitting or clinical prediction. Do not silently reactivate the failed old next-twelve branch.
3. **Clinical prediction launch and later inferential release:** launch requires qualified historical training/calibration, immutable prediction recipe, genuinely future origin, endpoint and reference route, planned sample size/assurance and pre-outcome freeze. Realized future outcomes are not required before prospective predictions; they are required before estimating performance or claiming adequately realized information. Separate the later analysis gate from the launch gate to avoid making a prospective design logically impossible.

**Finite artifact recommendation:** one new versioned JSON protocol plus a readable companion and signed/hash-bound review disposition. Required fields: status/permissions; parent hashes; scientific question and H1/H2 mapping; unit and target-to-product ambiguity; frame/source coverage; ordered sampling algorithm; all-member denominators; exposure; source/attempt caps; origin-only fields; endpoint rule; occurrence/ascertainment windows; two sealed source reviews and adjudication; dependency graph definitions; training/calibration inventory; planned statistical method; pass/revise/stop table; output hashes. This is a specification using existing tools, not a new runtime or data collection.

**Finite feasibility sampling recommendation:** if a twelve-item cap is retained, label twelve explicitly as an engineering workload choice, never a power calculation. Define the source frame without conditioning on a readable future report, eventual success, known readout or found protocol. Choose deterministic seeded ordering or probability sampling before sources are inspected; document which inference that design permits. Preserve every screened record and exclusion, then the fixed selected-member denominator, per-component readiness, joint readiness, rejected/unknown/inapplicable categories, route failures and measured effort. Preserve the old 128-member pipeline exposure and the separately exposed packs; excluding known follow-ups does not create an untouched holdout. A source-rich convenience fixture may test extraction but cannot estimate frame-wide yield.

**Measurable pilot disposition, proposed policy:** zero identity/membership/hash/boundary violations is mandatory; any unresolved violation stops the affected batch. Every admitted joint-ready member must satisfy every applicable mandatory source qualification with locators, and every selected member must receive a disposition. At least one completely qualified package establishes only that the route can work on this frame; it permits planning a separately sized feasibility study, not scaling automatically. Zero joint-ready packages after the frozen cap stops that acquisition route. Partial packages or reviewer conflicts produce `revise` with a named missing element; revisions must preserve failures and use a new version before further acquisition. Do not invent a 90% clinical yield threshold by copying B0. If a population yield decision is desired later, prespecify a justified target, probability sample and precision-based size; otherwise report counts only.

### Gaps
- No clinical positive/negative training set, calibrated scorer or representative readiness-yield estimate is established by the retained planning evidence. — [Clinical design plan](../../planning/Clinical%20Validation%20Design%20and%20Data%20Feasibility%20Plan%202026-10-22.md).
- Original H1 calls Brier co-primary but does not supply an explicit co-primary Brier success threshold or a fully reconciled multiplicity rule. Freeze that amendment before outputs; do not claim original H1 is operationally complete. — [Original proposal](../../planning/Beyond%20the%20Virtual%20Biotech%20Tooling%20Gaps%20and%20Project%20Plan.md).

## How should chronology, missingness, dependence and windows qualify?

### Takeaway
A future origin can remove future-outcome prediction leakage only when all contributing model assets, mappings, annotations and prediction rules are frozen before that origin. It does not supply historical training labels, solve selective publication, or turn unknown decisions into failures.

### Cited Findings
- The retained chronology checker checks immutable model revisions/releases and predictor-source dates, but explicitly reports an immutable-pre-origin-weight route rather than a verified training cutoff. It rejects true activation gates and requires retrospective freeze and twelve-month historical windows; it cannot certify a newly prospective study unchanged. — [Chronology validator](../../tools/feasibility/chronology_policy.py).
- Scorer preflight checks training/calibration label chronology, endpoint/horizon and scientific scope; T6 yields no clinical probability. The scoring qualification reviewer checks declarations and contradictions while returning `fit_allowed=false` on every path. — [Scorer](../../src/asof/scorer.py); [Qualification reviewer](../../tools/science/scoring_qualification.py).
- ICH E9(R1) distinguishes the scientific treatment-effect question, intercurrent events, missing observations and sensitivity analysis; its framework motivates explicit endpoint attributes and assumptions, not automatic replacement of missing outcomes. — [FDA final guidance](https://www.fda.gov/media/148473/download).
- A primary cohort study found that publication timing differed with result direction. Its old single-center results do not estimate current project latency, but establish why prompt public availability cannot be assumed independent of efficacy. — [Stern and Simes, original cohort study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9310565/).

### Inferences
**Project judgments: chronology contract.** Require an actual future origin, immutable revisions and local weight hashes for every scientific model role; prompts, tools, retrieval corpora, ontology/mapping ancestry, derived-feature recipe, aggregation, selection and abstention rules must be available and frozen by origin. A release date before origin is a defensible upper-bound route for those exact immutable weights, not proof of training-cutoff documentation or absence of memorization of earlier development cases. Later reviewers may inspect outcomes as evaluator-only agents; their knowledge cannot enter predictors or test membership. Retrospective development exposure remains disclosed even in a genuinely prospective evaluation.

Keep rule authenticity, prespecification, program/arm/population match, analysis identity, occurrence time and public-availability time as separate fields. A primary decision not yet made at origin may have an authentic preanalysis rule public after origin: that rule can support later evaluator adjudication, but cannot become an origin predictor. Decision dates that are only intervals qualify window inclusion only if the entire interval lies within the frozen occurrence interval; straddling boundaries remain indeterminate unless a prespecified interval-censoring method applies.

**Project judgments: window contract.** Do not assign calendar origin now. Specify the rule for choosing it after launch gates pass. Candidate design is a twelve-month occurrence horizon plus a separately frozen reporting allowance; no evidence here establishes three months, twelve months or any other allowance as sufficient for efficacy. Log decision occurrence, data cutoff, completion, source disclosure and retrieval independently. Late-disclosed in-window decisions belong to a prespecified later sensitivity tranche if outside the primary ascertainment deadline; later-occurring decisions never become primary in-window events. No extending the primary deadline after inspecting yield. A prospective prediction log can begin after launch gates pass and wait for bounded follow-up; a prediction log alone does not satisfy H1/H2.

**Project judgments: missingness and competing events.** Preserve at least efficacy success, efficacy failure under authentic rule, no decision yet with qualified observation, administrative censoring, access/ascertainment unknown, and non-efficacy competing stop. Unknown public evidence is not independent censoring by default. Conditional performance on observed decisions must be named and accompanied by all-member missingness and scope limits. A whole-frame fixed-horizon binary efficacy claim requires a defined outcome for every state or an explicitly justified missing-data/competing-event estimand; it cannot silently drop stops or impute failures. For an equal-weight event-proportion summary, report identification bounds `positive/N` to `(positive+unknown)/N` when the unknown mass is binary-compatible; do not call these performance bounds, apply them to competing events without an estimand, or confuse them with confidence intervals. Weighted frames need weighted mass. IPCW or imputation needs stated identification/positivity assumptions and sensitivity analysis; neither repairs unobserved negative ascertainment automatically.

**Project judgments: dependence.** Freeze relationships among repeated trial/arm/regimen, product, indication, sponsor and target before outcomes, distinguishing primary dependency components from sensitivity graphs. A target-level label cannot be cloned across product trials to increase event count. Preserve paired scores within the same member and resample independent components for uncertainty where justified. Indication-only bootstrap in the original plan must be reconciled with cross-indication product/target dependence before use. A giant connected component may make population inference infeasible; connected-component counts are diagnostics, not a claimed effective sample size.

### Gaps
- Reporting-latency distribution for a properly defined prospective frame is unmeasured. This cannot be supplied from the old purposive cases or from the publication-bias paper. Current work can state assumptions and sensitivity scenarios, but cannot claim a validated allowance. — [Clinical design plan](../../planning/Clinical%20Validation%20Design%20and%20Data%20Feasibility%20Plan%202026-10-22.md).
- No admissible training/calibration history or reference qualification for independent clinical truth is established. Two blinded agent reviews and separate adjudication are available for a source-grounded development reference; agreement alone does not certify therapeutic validity. No new human-panel requirement is introduced for writing or accepting the readiness protocol. — [Remaining gates](../../planning/Remaining%20Scientific%20Gates%20and%20Prospective%20Design%202026-10-23.md).

## What can be reused and completed now, and what must wait?

### Takeaway
Complete the protocol, source-only schema, gate matrix and reusable-tool compatibility review now. Keep sample acquisition, model dispatch, clinical calibration, cohort expansion and public registration outside this artifact's permissions.

### Cited Findings
- Registry power v0.2 uses independent synthetic Gaussian units, proxy recorded starts, a two-sided absolute paired-AUROC statistic and finite-rank null references. It explicitly disclaims clinical outcomes and independent sample-size qualification; v0.1's small-sample calibration failure is retained. — [Power protocol](../../planning/protocols/registry-power-v0.2.json); [Power implementation](../../tools/feasibility/registry_power.py).
- Existing origin and follow-up tools enforce particular old protocol identifiers/guards and expose source-binding or ancestry-verification functions. Reference assembly and panel tools already support bound artifacts and sealed review mechanics. — [Origin maturity](../../tools/science/origin_maturity.py); [Endpoint follow-up](../../tools/science/endpoint_followup.py); [Endpoint restore](../../tools/science/endpoint_restore.py); [Reference panel](../../tools/science/reference_panel.py); [Reference assembly](../../tools/science/finalize_reference.py).

### Inferences
**Project judgments: reusable building blocks, not blanket certification.** Reuse `asof.serde` hashes/contracts, ancestry verification and receipt conventions; reviewer sealing and adjudication artifact patterns; scorer applicability checks and fail-closed scoring-plan review; dependency-graph and paired-AUROC placement calculations for design discussion. Do not feed a new cohort through old exact-ID guards, alter the old inactive protocol, or treat declaration validation as payload/scientific validation. No new runtime is needed for the present deliverable.

**Project judgments: clinical statistical release.** Freeze clinical success unit and exact relation to original target–indication estimand; require qualified earlier outcomes and leakage-safe component-separated train/calibration/test histories. Predefine feature selection, preprocessing, missingness indicators, score fitting, held-out calibration and baseline calibration using earlier data only. A frozen uncalibrated ranking is a separate output claim; it cannot fulfill Brier co-primary as a probability model. No prospective origin can bypass this training prerequisite for the original AsOf scorer.

Design a future simulation specification using clinical success/failure/unknown counts and dependency structures rather than registry proxy totals: paired score distributions, baseline AUROC range, gain 0.05, observed-score correlation, cluster sizes, missingness correlated with outcome/scores, label error, abstentions, and competing events. Reconcile one-sided original H1 versus two-sided retained proxy simulation; define H2 and co-primary multiplicity and Brier criterion. Simulate the actual proposed component-respecting inference and validate null type-I behavior separately from power, report Monte Carlo uncertainty and invalid replicates. Proposed acceptance policy: target 80% power at 0.05 AUROC improvement under a justified conservative design scenario, with calibration/precision requirements separately specified; if these inputs cannot be justified, status is `not sized`, never `passed`. Training sample adequacy is a separate calculation. A hypothetical simulation can be specified now but cannot be presented as measured feasibility.

**Finite next actions possible now without waiting for future outcomes:** draft the versioned protocol and companion; list every field/evidence requirement; construct a blank denominator/disposition schema; prepare an exposure inventory from retained manifests; record the explicit incompatible assumptions of old tools; have agents independently review design and source-rule completeness, seal their reviews and reconcile discrepancies; validate local links, JSON syntax, source hashes and absence of clinical mutations; publish an internal accepted-design disposition with every scientific launch gate still false. A public preregistration would be a later external action tied to the actual qualified study and prediction digests, not an automatic effect of local acceptance. None of these tasks requires requesting a user file or inventing a paid-access dependency.

### Gaps
- An actual new sampling-frame inventory, qualified outcome yield, latency estimate and fitted scorer cannot be generated within the non-dispatch scope. These require separately frozen substantive work; only outcome follow-up itself intrinsically waits for the future. — [Remaining gates](../../planning/Remaining%20Scientific%20Gates%20and%20Prospective%20Design%202026-10-23.md).
- Methods sources on sample-size development/validation were found, but the BMJ full-text endpoints returned 403 during this review; no unseen detailed recommendation is relied upon. The accessible Pavlou original study supports the metric-specific sample-size point above, not a numeric project gate. — [Accessible original study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8529102/).
