# Biomedical benchmark priorities and transferable methods for TETHER

## 1. Which biomedical benchmarks can measure useful performance next?

### Takeaway

Keep the execution sequence: completed finite BG02 conformance comparison → actual SciFact → one original LAB-Bench family → equal-obligation Inspect. The biomedical priority is original LitQA2 or DbQA; R2E is a subsequent target-ranking extension. Newer gated or analytical benchmarks are useful conditional options, not prerequisites for the existing sequence. These are research recommendations, not newly executed benchmark results. [Current execution status](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/planning/TETHER Equal Policy Comparison Results 2026-11-05.md>)

### Cited Findings

- **R2E clinical labels are a different endpoint.** The released outcome subset labels phase-II/III-to-regulatory-approval progression, under CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 with stated Citeline permission for that subset. It is not an endpoint-success label, an unrestricted PharmaProjects license, or a substitute for dated clinical cohort construction. The paper uses clinical AUROC. [Release terms and fields](https://github.com/BenevolentAI/r2e-evaluation-data), [paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.167.pdf)
- **TargetBench is currently a methodology comparator, not the easiest executable benchmark.** The 2026 paper covers 38 diseases and multidimensional target-list assessment; source datasets are listed publicly, but custom code is proprietary and available on reasonable academic request. Authors explicitly warn that learned clinical-stage associations are not future clinical-success predictions and that text popularity can leak development status. [Primary article, limitations/data/code availability](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-47765-3)

### Inferences

- Choose LitQA2 if a prespecified access audit finds enough permitted complete source documents; choose DbQA if database versions/query results can be frozen more reliably. Keep the family choice independent of candidate accuracy. Cite coverage separately from accuracy and never reconstruct missing inputs from the gold key passage. This operational choice follows the official task schemas rather than a demonstrated relative TETHER gain. [Original card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/futurehouse/lab-bench/raw/main/README.md)
- A small public benchmark can establish answering utility under stated conditions while remaining unsuitable for unseen/generalized or clinical claims. The immediate paper does not need all five families, a new expert recruitment gate, or a licensed clinical database. Existing project sequencing already places one family after SciFact. [Execution plan](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/planning/TETHER Benchmark Execution Plan 2026-11-02.md>)

### Gaps

- No new dataset assets were downloaded, no native scorer was executed, and no candidate scientific answers were generated for this research. Complete article access, exact task revision hashes and benchmark license files must be established during the selected adapter's input inspection.
- GenomeArena's archive license and exact grader implementation remain unqualified. TargetBench's requested proprietary code is not available locally through this review. These are conditional access issues, not reasons to delay the existing open-task sequence.

## 2. Which peer methods deserve adoption, and which are already overlapping?

### Takeaway

The highest-value additions are native answer/evidence evaluation, semantic claim checking with bounded repair, and evidence/identity sensitivity experiments. Generic orchestration, provenance records and source verification already overlap with peers and should not be advertised as unique. Adoption proposals below are hypotheses to test; they are not transferred peer performance gains. [Existing capability audit](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/research_notes/TETHER benchmark fit and advances/current_project_gap_map.md>)

### Cited Findings

- **Virtual Biotech now explicitly overlaps on audit infrastructure.** Current official documentation describes manifests/configuration, claims/provenance, trace/cost logs, separate artifact-integrity and evidence-coverage checks, incomplete results when claims or data checks fail, and audit commands without model calls. It distinguishes recorded-script reruns from fresh model replay and cautions that these checks do not establish scientific correctness. The manifest implementation records producing agent/tool call and artifact hashes. These are current repository features, not proof that the project's older pinned paper revision contained them. [Current official README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harrisongzhang/TheVirtualBiotech/main/README.md), [manifest implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harrisongzhang/TheVirtualBiotech/main/src/utils/run_manifest.py)
- **R2E provides an evidence-first ranking design:** it masks candidate identity, learns retrieval/scoring, and attributes answer scores to evidence using Shapley values. Its reported comparison and architectural ablations are specific to its tasks. Relevant backward lineage is explicit: R2E cites retrieval-augmented question answering and Shapley/SHAP attribution; this does not make TETHER a demonstrated descendant. [R2E introduction and ablations](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.167.pdf)
- **Alvessa describes statement-level validation plus revision:** retrieved database records support claims, and unsupported statements trigger feedback. This is an explicit architectural claim, not a verified gain from this read. [Author README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ksenia007/alvessa_agent/main/README.md)
- **BiomniBench separates process quality from final outputs:** its supplied reference traces and rubrics make analytical decisions assessable. Automatically scoring a recorded trace is still judge-dependent. [Official task layout](https://huggingface.co/datasets/phylobio/BiomniBench-DA)
- **Documented successor chain:** original LAB-Bench (2024) → LABBench2 (2026), established by the successor's own repository. Relationships among TETHER, R2E, Alvessa and LAB benchmarks here are methodological comparisons, not invented citation edges. [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10362), [successor repository](https://github.com/EdisonScientific/labbench2)

### Inferences

- Do not rebuild provenance or multi-agent roles merely to match these peers. Establish whether the particular temporal, hypothesis, permission and ancestor rules add useful discrimination under equal policy. Current Virtual Biotech overlap makes a generic “auditable biotech agents” novelty claim substantially weaker. [Virtual Biotech documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harrisongzhang/TheVirtualBiotech/main/README.md), [TETHER equal-policy outcome](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/planning/TETHER Equal Policy Comparison Results 2026-11-05.md>)
- Prefer simple source-removal controls before learned evidence attribution. They are easier to audit and can falsify dependence on the provided evidence without introducing a new opaque score. This is an experiment-design recommendation, not a result.

### Gaps

- The current Virtual Biotech repository was inspected as public source, not installed or executed. No adversarial test established equivalence to TETHER's complete rule set. Its current HEAD, the paper system and commit `71f9da68c95c5ee0ef4c1d9b8f45f10693de022d` must remain distinct comparator versions.
- **Version-pin limitation, checked November 5, 2026:** the web reader returned the live `main` README/manifest but could not fetch GitHub's commit API; a direct read-only API request failed DNS. Its cached commits page showed only an August 17 entry and cannot reliably pin the newer inspected content. Therefore no exact current-main SHA/date is asserted. The overlap is documentation plus inspected manifest-source evidence, not an installed or version-pinned equivalence result. A future executable comparison must resolve and archive its commit first.
- Capability status above is scoped to retained source audits and the current completion report; it is not an exhaustive claim that no related utility exists anywhere in the repository. Parent code-state review should reconcile concurrent work before report publication.

## 3. How should completed BG02 change the argument and evaluation design?

### Takeaway

BG02 establishes a bounded engineering result and exposes a repaired defect; it does not establish TETHER scientific performance or superiority to an equally specified implementation. The next discriminating result is useful, source-supported task completion under matched conditions. [Completed BG02 report](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/planning/TETHER Equal Policy Comparison Results 2026-11-05.md>)

### Cited Findings

- Corrected TETHER and the independent full-policy implementation conform on **109/109 exposed authored cases**; original TETHER conforms on **108/109**, falsely admitting a response linked to a wrong request. Each version has **109 × 4 × 3 = 1,308 terminal cells**. Repeats are not extra independent cases. [Original reconstruction summary](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/planning/review-assets/tether-equal-policy-v2-20261105/reconstruction/OLD-RESULT-SUMMARY-20261105.json>), [patched summary](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/planning/review-assets/tether-equal-policy-v2-20261105/reconstruction/PATCHED-RESULT-SUMMARY-20261105.json>)
- The patched median within-case import-plus-operation ratio is **1.65 times** the independent comparator; this is adverse cost evidence within the measured fresh-process setup, not warm-service scaling. The comparator is an independently implemented local control, not a published external framework run. [Patched reconstruction](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/planning/review-assets/tether-equal-policy-v2-20261105/reconstruction/PATCHED-RESULT-SUMMARY-20261105.json>)
- The earlier report's “BG02 unscored” checkpoint is now superseded, not retroactively erroneous. Its scientific-answering gap and ordered next tasks remain: BG03 SciFact, BG04 original LAB-Bench, BG05 Inspect. [Earlier report](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/reports/TETHER benchmark fit and advances.md>), [current status](<D:/Projects/drug_target_agents/planning/TETHER Equal Policy Comparison Results 2026-11-05.md>)

### Inferences

- Report two complementary experiments: fixed candidate artifacts for the gate's causal effect, and the same answer producer/evidence/tools/budget for end-to-end utility. A larger or newer model in one arm would confound framework value.
- Retain **all scheduled tasks** in the denominator. Separately report native accuracy, source/rationale correctness, correct-and-admitted yield, false rejection, unsupported admission, abstention, input unavailability, failures and cost. “Unsupported admission” needs a benchmark support reference; a merely incorrect answer can be provenance-valid.
- Add selective risk versus coverage after a separate calibration split, and stratify by source/task family rather than treating repeated runs as independent evidence. Repeat stochastic answering with prespecified seeds; the deterministic BG02 repetitions are timing/replay observations. These are proposed analyses, not calibrated TETHER results.
- Keep public dev exposure visible. Freeze task/source/grader revisions; isolate gold answers and reference traces; retain unsuccessful attempts rather than replacing their denominator. Study-level duplicates and shared evidence should not be counted as independent support. These controls strengthen an honest limited claim without reinstating an external expert-input gate.

### Gaps

- No matched natural scientific failure prevalence, prospective clinical calibration, native benchmark answer result, or independently blind generalization result follows from BG02. None was produced by this research.
- Bounded peer-read of the root BG02 result report against both reconstruction summaries found no numerical or conformance/cost interpretation error. Release-test counts and commit-attestation details were outside that bounded read; their own retained evidence remains authoritative.
