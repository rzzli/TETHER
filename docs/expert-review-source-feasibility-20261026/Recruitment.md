# Invitation: source-only evidence feasibility review

We invite reviewers with relevant immunology, psoriasis/IBD, genetics or evidence-appraisal expertise to assess whether a small set of supplied excerpts supports a limited target–disease reporting question. The two contexts are BTK/psoriasis and CTLA4/Crohn's disease. You may select outside my expertise for either context or item.

The offline form contains eight passage occurrences from six article identifiers and seven multiple-choice items per context. Please record your actual review time; no duration or professional performance threshold is imposed. Optional comments, confidence, expertise, conflicts and prior exposure help interpret your feedback.

Please complete the source-only reference independently before receiving candidate responses, AI assessments or another reviewer's answers. No external research is required. Return the exported JSON via the coordinator's agreed channel. Scheduling and any compensation are arranged directly with the coordinator.

This is development feasibility work, not clinical guidance or a validated benchmark. A completed form contributes a source-only judgment; it does not by itself complete reviewer coverage or adjudication.
