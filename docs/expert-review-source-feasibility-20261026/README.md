# Source-only feasibility review

Open `Expert-source-review.html` in a browser. It works offline. Read each source excerpt and answer seven short items for each of two contexts. All answers are optional; cannot-assess and outside-expertise choices are available. No score or pass/fail outcome is assigned.

Complete this reference before viewing candidate responses or AI assessments. Record prior exposure honestly. The material has six article identifiers and eight passage occurrences; repeated text is retained and does not represent another independent study. Action, modality and tissue are unassigned.

Export the review JSON before closing. Drafts are not automatically saved. Return it through your coordinator's agreed channel; the form sends nothing. Exported answers are evaluation-only and unadjudicated. Blank forms are not human responses, and these contexts are not certified fresh cases.

Source references and dates reproduce supplied metadata; no external lookup is needed. Preserve literal source wording in your assessment. Report important missing information rather than assuming it.

This instrument supports development source-feasibility review. It does not
establish a fresh benchmark or authorize candidate generation. A fixed article
identifier also occurs in prior project material; the selected passages have
been retained without replacement. Please disclose your own previous exposure.

Desktop/mobile offline checks pass for blank answers, exact escaped source text,
expanded original locators and downloaded JSON readback. The QA files are dummy
exports and do not count as reviewer submissions. Nothing is transmitted.
