## 2. Recruitment and independence

For this exercise seek relevant experience in RA, IL-6/IL6R biology, translational immunology or antibody pharmacology. Confirm the reviewer can judge the supplied context; years of employment or academic title alone do not establish topic competence. Record role, relevant experience, conflicts, prior exposure to this case/project and any authorship or commercial connection to the supplied evidence.

Arrange two independent qualified submissions. Ask a third relevant expert to be available for substantive disagreements. Do not assume the same people cover future oncology, metabolic, ion-channel or statistical-genetics cases. Narrow the study's domain if panel coverage is limited.

Do not grade professional competence by agreement with an AI answer key. Correctly identifying uncertainty, a problematic source statement or an ambiguous candidate is useful evidence. A person may be highly confident that a question cannot be assessed from the packet.

The invitation's 30–45 minutes is an unmeasured scheduling estimate for this orientation exercise. Record actual minutes and reviewer feedback before promising time for the full study. Compensation and scheduling are owner-arranged; no expense is authorized by these files.

## 3. Administer the development exercise

1. Give each reviewer a fresh blank file and unique reviewer ID. Record which packet version/hash was sent.
2. Ask the reviewer to use only the supplied evidence first. An optional later external-knowledge comment is separate from source-only assessment.
3. In the HTML, complete Part 1 and preserve the source-only answers before revealing the candidate. This is a procedural aid, not a security boundary: the static HTML contains all exercise content. The PDF also contains both stages; ask the reviewer to stop at the indicated break and save their first-stage answers before continuing.
4. Collect the exported Part 1 record where practical, then the full answers. Preserve both originals. Do not fill unanswered items or translate uncertainty into an error.
5. Record previous exposure, elapsed time, source/candidate stages, outside-expertise items, confidence and lookup disclosures. If a reviewer sees the candidate first, label the reference candidate-exposed; do not claim it was constructed independently of the output.
6. Check only completeness and file identity before the second reviewer submits. Do not tell a reviewer which responses the project expects.
7. Discuss comprehension or interface problems after independent submissions. Revisions create a new instrument version; prior responses remain bound to the old version.

No automatic scoring or pass/fail result appears in the expert form. Source-reading questions orient reviewers; the scientific judgments are the actual work.

### Candidate judgments to keep separate

- **Proposition support:** is the statement supported anywhere in the supplied material?
- **Citation entailment:** does its actual linked passage support it? A proposition can be supported elsewhere while its citation is inadequate.
- **Context preservation:** are model, exposure, comparator and endpoint preserved? Missing dose/timing does not erase a reported cell context.
- **Applicability:** disease/action alignment is not proof of patient benefit. Missingness does not by itself prove a mismatch.
- **Underlying science:** a secondary review can contain an inaccurate or incomplete mechanistic statement. Faithful copying cannot validate it.
- **Utility and omissions:** a cautious answer with no critical falsehood can still omit the information needed to answer the question.

The current candidate's first linked passage discusses perspectives rather than explicitly nominating a target. Other supplied passages contain target nomination. Its second passage supports a conditional antibody-development proposal. Those observations guide discussion, but do not decide ambiguous wording or overall utility for an expert.

## 5. Preserve disagreement and adjudicate

After both independent forms are received, compare ratings dimension by dimension. Preserve original choice, confidence, evidence pointer, rationale and outside-expertise response. Do not overwrite them with a consensus label.

Ask a third qualified reviewer to resolve consequential disagreements with the source material and the two rationales. Give the adjudicator no model identity or project-preferred answer. The final record should include the disputed proposition/span, both original ratings, adjudicator disposition, rationale, evidence and unresolved uncertainty. A disagreement may reveal a source or question defect that should remain unresolved.

For the ambiguous context limitation, do not force agreement that it unequivocally denies all context. The expert can judge it too broad, ambiguous, justified for particular missing details or otherwise explain a defensible reading. An unsupported assertion and a contradicted assertion are not interchangeable categories.

The orientation exercise's timing, ambiguity and workload findings inform a new instrument. They cannot estimate general dossier accuracy, clinical success or model superiority.

## 6. Later fresh evaluation procedure

Before any confirmatory H5 use:

1. Select genuinely fresh cases and a domain-matched panel. Exclude the current exposed case and document all prior case/output exposure.
2. Freeze source packets, available-information dates, source limitations and important evidence units. Each reviewer first constructs a source-only reference, including reported result/proposal distinctions and uncertainties.
3. Seal independent references before showing model outputs. If references are later corrected, retain both versions and disclose whether the correction was candidate-informed.
4. Generate all arms under the declared fixed-evidence or end-to-end design; seal outputs and failed attempts before review. Never let an expert's reference become a model input.
5. Randomize arm order and neutral labels per case. Normalize only presentation; preserve caveats, meaning, omissions and exact source pointers. Keep the arm key evaluator-only.
6. Obtain two independent relevant ratings per case and third-reviewer adjudication. Permit ties, cannot-assess and outside-expertise responses. Record identity guesses if masking may have failed.
7. Analyze paired outcomes at the case/declared cluster level. Claims and reviewers are not independent extra cases. Report pre-adjudication agreement, disagreement, missingness and useful coverage.
8. Include failures and abstentions in operational denominators. Distinguish zero errors because no answer was produced from a useful accurate answer.
9. Size and preregister the final evaluation using pilot variability and workload. The original six-expert/forty-pair H5 design and the new two-reviewer orientation exercise have different scopes.

Human review supports independent scientific judgment about the supplied evidence. It does not prove experimental replication, therapeutic efficacy, clinical-label validity or causal genetic direction. Those require their own qualified evidence and methods.
