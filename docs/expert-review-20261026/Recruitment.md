# Invitation to review scientific evidence summaries

We are evaluating whether computational research summaries describe supplied biological evidence accurately, preserve experimental context and acknowledge uncertainty. We seek reviewers with relevant experience in rheumatoid arthritis, IL-6/IL6R biology, translational immunology or antibody pharmacology.

The initial exercise concerns **IL6R inhibition with an antibody for rheumatoid arthritis**. You will read a small set of attributed source excerpts, answer multiple-choice questions and assess a short anonymous research summary. This is a review of the evidence and summary, not an examination of your professional competence or a request to endorse a treatment.

**What to expect**

- A self-contained offline form. No installation, account or required internet research.
- A first section about the supplied evidence, followed by a separate candidate-summary assessment.
- Options for uncertainty and questions outside your expertise, with room for comments.
- An initial scheduling estimate of 30–45 minutes. We will use your actual time and feedback to improve the review process.
- An independent submission: please do not consult another reviewer's responses before completing yours.

Please tell the coordinator your relevant expertise, any conflicts, whether you have seen this project or example before, and whether you prefer attributed or pseudonymous credit. Review arrangements, timing and any compensation should be agreed directly with the coordinator before a larger study.

Open **Expert-review.html** in a browser, or complete **Expert-review.pdf** digitally or on paper. The HTML can export a JSON answer file for you to return through your usual agreed channel. It does not upload answers. Save/export before closing, particularly if your browser disables local draft storage. Paper/PDF reviewers can return the annotated form.

The exercise is development material. It does not establish clinical benefit or a clinical prediction benchmark. A later study, if feasible, will use fresh cases and separately agreed review assignments.
