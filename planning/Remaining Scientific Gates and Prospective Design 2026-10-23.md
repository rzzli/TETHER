## Prospective clinical design: proposed contract

This is the design branch already contemplated in the October 22 plan. It is not
a registered protocol, a frozen prediction set or an activated follow-up study.
No actual new origin date is assigned retrospectively.

1. **Question and unit.** Retain the original clinical efficacy question separately
   from registration, reporting and actual dosing. Define one experimental
   product/regimen × indication/population × origin phase. Freeze target/action/
   modality/tissue annotations separately. Do not equate a multi-target product
   outcome with efficacy of each target.
2. **Origin-only readiness.** Before selecting a new evaluation cohort, specify a
   source-defined sampling frame and require authentic prespecified efficacy rules,
   identifiable program/population, origin eligibility and accessible source
   provenance. Record unavailable rules and ambiguous mappings in the denominator.
   A source-rich convenience set may be a methods fixture, but cannot be advertised
   as representative or used to estimate population label yield.
3. **Prediction freeze.** Choose a genuinely future origin only after readiness
   passes. Pin all models, weights, prompts, tools, retrieval inputs, annotations,
   mappings and aggregation rules. Archive predictions and abstentions before any
   follow-up. Disclose retrospective design and model-family exposure. Unverified
   training cutoff remains a limitation even if model release precedes origin.
4. **Endpoint and timing.** Freeze a named clinical decision and its authentic
   success/failure rule, occurrence window and separate public-ascertainment window.
   A 12-month horizon plus a separately justified reporting allowance is a candidate,
   not a commitment. Reporting dates, trial completion, new registration and dosing
   cannot substitute for an efficacy decision. Missing decisions remain unknown;
   stop reasons and competing events retain their own classes.
5. **Independent reference.** Reference authors must not see predictions. Two sealed
   reviews plus separate adjudication can be implemented by agents for development,
   but shared source/model bias and missing therapeutic truth remain explicit.
   Claims requiring independent clinical validation require a reference qualified
   for that claim; multiplying agreeing agents does not supply it automatically.
6. **Sample size and analysis.** First measure qualified outcomes and dependency
   components, then simulate the actual frozen metric under missingness, event
   balance, pairing and clustering. Use the original clinical effect/precision
   objective where applicable; do not copy a registry count or source-ranking rate
   into a clinical power calculation. No invented effective sample size or current
   claim of adequate power. Freeze exclusions, multiplicity and stopping rules.
7. **Scoring and comparators.** Qualify historical training/calibration separately
   from future evaluation. Keep probabilities null until a validated derivation and
   fitted/calibrated scorer exist. Define comparator data access, model, evidence,
   budget and adaptation limits before outputs; the present role adapter is not a
   reproduction of the complete Virtual Biotech system.
8. **Registration and later ascertainment.** A public preregistration, if used, must
   precede outcomes and bind the actual protocol/prediction digests. Local Git history
   is not public registration. Later follow-up is bounded by the new protocol; do not
   launch background monitoring or download work from this design document.

## Execution disposition

Finish and seal the current T6 development evaluation, original-row audit, status
report and backup. Then retain the above scientific gates as open. The next
clinical deliverable would be a **non-dispatch, endpoint-specific readiness
protocol** with a source-defined frame, an admissible independence/reference route,
and explicit pass/stop criteria before any new trial extraction or search.

Do not start B1 automatically: it needs a separately frozen origin linkage and
candidate-universe coverage audit. Do not optimize the current sealed T6 recipe
after seeing its answers. Any future performance work must preserve complete
ancestry and mutation checks and use a separately versioned execution recipe.

Completion of this disposition means the remaining work is identified, not that
Tasks 6–8 are complete. Future outcomes and independent scientific truth cannot be
created by engineering retries. No clinical score, new cohort, paid access, new
download, live provider adapter or public registration is introduced here.
