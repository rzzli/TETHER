## Accepted outcome and its limits

Version 0.3.11 introduces `source-quote-interpretation-v3-gbnf` and deterministic
`strict-interpretation-gbnf-v1` preparation. It builds finite, shared grammar
productions while preserving decoded scalar values, joint nonblank/length limits,
answer states, exact fields/counts, source-literal/quote-ID pairing and either order
of distinct references. It binds grammar, serialization-domain, acceptance-schema,
request, catalog and payload fingerprints. Original response values and the complete
strict post-validator are retained. Whole-response bytes, global claim-text uniqueness,
source/history authority and scientific entailment remain separate checks.

The new interface is preparation-only: `execution_authorized` is false, features
remain empty and clinical probabilities null. **The default controller/native route
remains v2.** V3 has not been wired into dispatch or qualified by actual document/EOG
tests. The [API documentation](../docs/INTERPRETATION_GENERATION.md),
[contract](https://github.com/rzzli/TETHER/blob/tether-pre-namespace-migration-20261106/planning/review-assets/recovery-step9-generation-contract-20261028/CONTRACT.md) and
[bounded-input amendment](https://github.com/rzzli/TETHER/blob/tether-pre-namespace-migration-20261106/planning/review-assets/recovery-step9-generation-contract-20261028/AMENDMENT-1-BOUNDED-INPUT.md)
describe the accepted boundary.

  and package/demo checks; an offline wheel with **85 runtime/resource members**,
  two installed environments and a dependency-free default runtime. PyArrow 21.0.0
  remains an optional ingestion dependency.
- Default installed verification of seven legacy families, **12 v3 stages,

- [Eighteen retained preparations](https://github.com/rzzli/TETHER/blob/tether-pre-namespace-migration-20261106/planning/review-assets/recovery-step9-generation-contract-20261028/PREPARATION-QUALIFICATION.json):

## Data, chronology and scientific feasibility

This milestone acquired no biological dataset, clinical label, target hypothesis,
paid access or new model. Existing collected evidence and restored clinical records
support bounded development, but catalog entries alone do not establish access,
complete parsing, license/redistribution rights or scientific validity. Selected
pilot storage does not size the complete inventory. Restricted products and missing
historical releases remain explicit dependencies; later annotations cannot silently
fill historical gaps.

The exposed IL6R material still has five literature candidates, one selected whole
record, nine available observations and 65 missing inputs. Both current policies
select that record. This establishes neither retrieval superiority nor sufficient
genetic-study evidence. Shared article identities, exposure, date discrepancies,
unspecified tissue and unknown context remain unresolved. Development exposure
precludes treating these preparations as fresh held-out performance cases.

Publication, public availability, annotation version, retrieval date and computation
time must remain distinct. Availability today proves neither historical eligibility
nor a model-training cutoff. Registry status, efficacy, approval and sponsor
continuation are different endpoints. The retained clinical feasibility reports show
[36 unqualified endpoint labels](Endpoint-specific%20Follow-up%20Feasibility%202026-10-21.md),
[17/32 observable registry primary states](B0%20Registry%20Content%20Feasibility%202026-10-22.md)
and [0/12 jointly source-ready cases](Source%20Readiness%20Closeout%20and%20T6%20Quality%20Next%20Stage%202026-10-24.md).
Those are substantive limitations, not problems solved by grammar work or more
unscoped AACT downloads. Original AUROC improvement 0.05 and co-primary Brier
requirements remain unless prospectively amended.

Genetics still requires complete estimator-appropriate regional data, assay identity,
build/allele harmonization, uncertainty, sample size, ancestry/LD compatibility,
cohort-overlap handling and defensible intervention interpretation. No causal feature,
clinical scorer, calibration or biological conclusion is qualified here. Human
references and source sufficiency remain necessary for independent H5 claims;
AI engineering or literature assessments cannot supply human submissions.

   Declare each next experiment's exact assets, scope, labels, limits and stop conditions.
2. Close the exact public C/GGML header and loader-source dependencies before building
   a typed recognizer. Do not guess layouts, cast private sampler state or substitute
   another DLL, converter mode, vocabulary or easier corpus.
3. Qualify the approved **CPU vocabulary/metadata-only** route under its own protocol:
   establish loader early return, backend/device behavior, split-GGUF handling and
   measured isolation. A null device pointer can mean all devices. This creates a
   vocabulary-owning model handle; metadata reads and hashing weight-file bytes are
   distinct from allocating tensors, creating a context or running inference.
4. Test exact token-piece byte roundtrip, next-token rejection and every vocabulary
   EOG ID for complete documents and declared incomplete prefixes. Character EOF,
   non-null sampler initialization, process exit zero and singleton membership are
   insufficient evidence. Retain parser-edge and post-validation exceptions explicitly.
5. Measure native full-vocabulary masking, prefix-stack growth and worst boundary
   strings under bounded counts/stops. Compact acyclic grammar does not prove low
   native runtime cost. Record time, memory, mappings, device behavior and cleanup.
6. Before **any integrated native case**, profile the preserved
   [295.922/300-second simulation](https://github.com/rzzli/TETHER/blob/tether-pre-namespace-migration-20261106/planning/review-assets/recovery-step9-native-duration-20261028/RESOURCE-RISK-NOTE.md):
   only 4.078 seconds remained without actual startup/inference. Separate lease/hash,
   preparation, tokenization, reservations, transport, cleanup and sealing costs.
   **Do not raise case budgets**, weaken integrity checks, choose an easier case or
   repeat until favorable timing appears. Any correction needs prospective scope,
   meaningful validation and installed replay before a once-only native experiment.
7. Continue fresh-source/exposure preparation and HR1–HR3 independently of these
   engineering gates. Activate R10 only after the frame, source references, human
   coverage and mechanics qualify; then size/preregister R11 and conditionally lock
   R12. R13–R16 retain their own scientific/data gates. Native mechanics alone cannot
   activate them or complete the original H1–H6 programme.
