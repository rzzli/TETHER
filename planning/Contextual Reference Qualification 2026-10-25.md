# IL6R contextual-reference qualification

This phase qualifies a development representation and its limitations, rather than
estimating general scientific accuracy. The exposed IL6R dossier is not a holdout.
The source surface and contract are already frozen in commit `693deb9`; the
verified checkpoint backup is recorded in commit `80f5809`.

## Inputs and independent proposals

Use the existing 43,527-byte dossier, its original-source binding and the sealed
172-line/1,158-node surface. Retain all nine source observations, selected-subset
coverage and the 65 missing collection inputs. Do not add current annotations or
reinterpret routing zeros as biological absence.

Two separately tasked AI reviewers propose contextual inventories without reading
each other's outputs. A third proposes evidence-dimension requirements without
reading either new inventory. Reviewers disclose inherited exposure to previous
development conclusions. This is separate AI reasoning with shared model and
training risks, not blinded human review or independent scientific truth.

Each proposal must preserve exact fragments, source attribution, context,
negation/modal force, numerical orientation, unresolved entities and dependence.
Every physical line and provider JSON value receives a disposition. A successful
schema check establishes representational consistency only. Draft status remains
valid when scientific or extraction uncertainty persists; no pressure to manufacture
a complete inventory is permitted.

Clinical efficacy, safety and outcome assertions remain clinical-unassessable.
This phase introduces no clinical labels, numerical prediction features, model
adapter calls, paid access or retries of the stopped registry route. Existing
primary-source research may inform subsequent adjudication if explicitly pinned
and disclosed; it cannot establish historical availability of model inputs.

## Freeze before comparison

Pin each reviewer's input bytes and final output bytes before cross-review.
Preserve original proposals and failed validation attempts. Comparison/adjudication
must write a fresh directory and record the exact proposal seals it received.
These development seals are not a retrospective preregistration or pre-prediction
freeze for an evaluation: the dossier and earlier conclusions were already exposed.

Review agreements as well as disagreements. Compare at source occurrence and
contextual proposition level, without treating unequal atom counts as accuracy.
Record split/merge differences, unresolved subject references, discovery versus
replication, anti-ligand versus anti-receptor distinctions, and unsigned source
metrics. A compound sentence can require several proposals; a numerical estimate,
interval and P value remain one qualified bundle. Repeated excerpts do not create
independent experiments.

## Evidence-dimension reference

For each source-only question dimension, record its requirement, supplied source
locations, relevant context, contradiction or uncertainty, and disposition:
addressed within supplied scope, partially addressed, unknown, or out of scope.
Keep desired evidence distinct from collected evidence. The reference must cover
target identity, direction and mechanism, intervention/modality applicability,
population/tissue/model context, genetic orientation and limitations, source
dependence, counterevidence, missingness and justified abstention. Quarantine
clinical truth rather than counting abstention as supported scientific evidence.

This is an evidence-dimension rubric, not a claim that all necessary real-world
evidence has been collected. Evidence absence requires a justified search scope;
incomplete collection alone cannot support it.

## Validation and acceptance

1. Check pinned inputs and final outputs; replay the original-source binding.
2. Validate both proposed inventories with the frozen v2 contract. Retain failures.
3. Audit fragment/context/numerical correspondence and full dispositions independently.
4. Freeze proposals, then retain an adjudicated inventory and a reconciliation of
   both inventories and the independently proposed completeness requirements.
5. Run source-inclusive v2 checks for any resulting review and scoped references.
   Reference qualification is an identified AI judgment, not a truth certificate.
6. Retain resource/accounting limits, uncertainty and a precise completion decision.

If the evidence cannot support exhaustive qualified extraction or reference
completeness, retain a draft and document the blocker. Do not publish a scientific
error percentage, independent sample size or zero-error victory. Scientific
validity and applicability remain separate from source fidelity.

Only after this development boundary is assessed should the project enforce a
fresh reference/prediction freeze, qualify bounded interpretation, and prepare a
fresh paired feasibility study. Full T6/H5 and original Tasks 6–8 remain open.
