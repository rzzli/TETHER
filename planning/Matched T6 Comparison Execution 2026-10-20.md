## Matched ceilings, accounting and failure behavior

Each case/arm shares ceilings of sixteen calls, 32,000 input tokens, 8,000 output
tokens, $0 provider spend and 300 seconds. Generation caps are 256 output tokens;
seed is 20261017 and temperature zero. The frozen deterministic arm rotation is
set before dispatch. These settings do not guarantee bitwise reproducibility.

Equal ceilings do not imply equal consumption or full-category completion. The
five-role adaptation can exhaust a budget earlier than a single-specialist arm;
actual calls, measured versus conservatively estimated usage, elapsed resources,
partial coverage and explicit abstentions remain reportable outcomes. The retained
retry ceiling does not authorize automatic repeats of failed/timed-out calls.

Failed or interrupted arm execution stops the launcher before any subsequent arm.
Its outputs, verification, journal and partial execution report remain inspectable;
the exclusive execution reservation cannot be reused. Budget exhaustion is a valid
planned disposition and can be followed by subsequent scheduled arms. Fresh
ablations cannot reuse the full arm's responses or merely mask its selected IDs.
