# Project progress reassessment: R09 integration checkpoint

**Snapshot:** 28 October 2026, 03:15 UTC / **27 October 2026, 20:15 PDT**.
**Role:** AI engineering and research-planning self-assessment. This is not an
independent human scientific review. It was prepared after the accepted 0.3.9
integration milestone, while its separately versioned 0.3.10 duration correction
was undergoing full validation. Later acceptance records supersede this dated
status; a running check is not counted as a result here.

The controlling scope remains the [sixteen-step recovery plan](Project%20Recovery%20and%20Evaluation%20Plan%202026-10-26.md),
the [recorded phase decision](decisions/2026-10-26-phase-scope.md) and the
[live TODO](../TODO.md). The immediate scientific objective is a bounded,
independently reviewed contemporary T6 evidence-interpretation pilot, followed
by an appropriately sized H5 study of dossier quality, errors and cost if feasible.
This does not replace the original clinical, genetics or other task branches.

## Assessment

The project has made substantial, reproducible engineering progress. It can
authenticate selected original evidence, enforce temporal and evaluation-role
boundaries, preserve whole records, account for bounded work, retain failures and
verify run bundles with explicit original-input capabilities. The native adapter
is connected to the existing controller and is tested with simulated transport.
This is more than a proposed architecture or a disconnected backend.

The project is **not yet ready for a scientific performance claim**. The negative
R07 diagnostic remains negative; no fresh evaluation frame has qualified; source
sufficiency and domain coverage are unresolved; independent human submissions are
still pending. Current fixtures do not establish model answer quality, useful
retrieval, causal genetics, calibrated clinical probabilities or superiority to
Virtual Biotech. Whole R09 and R10–R16 remain open.

The important planning correction is to keep engineering acceptance and scientific
readiness separate without making missing reviewers a blanket engineering stop.
The present technical sequence is justified, but must remain finite: close the
0.3.10 correction, test the actual pinned converter under a frozen protocol, and
report an unsupported/negative result if that is what occurs. More green tests
cannot supply independent scientific judgments or fresh cases.

## What has been proved at this checkpoint

The [0.3.9 acceptance](review-assets/recovery-step9-native-integration-20261027/acceptance.json)
records 1,724 full tests without failures, errors or skips, required probes and
package/demo checks, 324 unchanged code/resource/test pins, seven installed legacy
families and eight preserved matching study verifiers. Its
[measured closeout](review-assets/recovery-step9-native-integration-20261027/CLOSEOUT.md)
retains an actual-format exposed IL6R/RA/inhibit/antibody case with five literature
candidates, one selected whole record, nine available observations and 65 missing
inputs. Six actual offline tokenizer children, six simulated native stages and
two pure results complete in 200.016 seconds, reserving 24,558 input and 6,144
output tokens under the unchanged 300-second case limit. Source/model-inclusive
installed replay and guarded run-copy relocation pass. The guards require explicit
approved source/model roots and deny live repository code, original-run reads in
the relocated check, archived execution and dispatch.

These results prove the stated access, binding, lifecycle, accounting and replay
mechanics for the tested case and fixtures. They do **not** prove that the selected
annotation answers the inherited genetic-study question, that one selected record
is sufficient scientific coverage, or that the native model can finish all stages
inside the remaining time. The simulated process has no real startup/inference
cost. Parent peak working set was 533,012,480 bytes; tokenizer-child and GPU peaks
were not measured. The 200-second observation cannot be turned into a real-model
resource guarantee or a general speed advantage.

Run relocation is also narrower than full restoration: original raw/model inputs
remain available through caller-approved roots. It is not an independent backup
or proof that the entire source collection can be restored elsewhere. The
dependency-free runtime and optional byte-verified Arrow21 replay environment
remain separate. Provider spending for this milestone is $0; unmetered external
AI preparation and future human time are not thereby zero.

## Project-wide gaps that engineering acceptance does not close

**Evidence coverage and freshness remain the immediate scientific constraint.**
R08 verified 112 originals/8.22 GB and retained 440 date disagreements, but these
are scoped metadata/source checks. Twenty historical hypotheses across 18 targets
are already exposed. Of 56 preliminary combinations, 31 have conservative Virtual
Biotech mapping overlaps; the other 25 are not certified fresh. The fixed two-context
source preparation has six articles, eight passage occurrences and seven unique
texts, with shared article identity against earlier material. Article/row counts
are not independent scientific studies, and nonmatches do not prove freshness.
These findings prevent promoting the current frame into an untouched test.

The five-record IL6R access check also cannot establish useful retrieval: both
unchanged policies choose the same one whole record under the byte limit. Two
candidates are oversized alone; the remaining candidates cannot pair with the
selected record within the bound. There is no observed policy-quality advantage.
Missing collection/context cannot be described as biological absence. Full case
and source context, disease match, intervention action, modality and unspecified
tissue must remain visible to reviewers; apparent quotation fidelity is not an
answer to an inapplicable scientific question.

**The current study cannot yet support an H5 quality/error/cost conclusion.**
There is no qualified fresh frame, independent reference denominator, reviewer
agreement estimate or timed pilot burden. Repeated claims and outputs within a
case are dependent; they cannot inflate the sample size. A four-arm role
adaptation of Virtual Biotech must be described honestly with its changed tools,
data access and budget. Offline operation and source cutoffs do not independently
prove absence of parametric model knowledge or historical label leakage.

**Clinical and causal branches remain substantive research, not implementation
cleanup.** Earlier work is preserved and should not be described as never
attempted. The [fixed endpoint follow-up](Endpoint-specific%20Follow-up%20Feasibility%202026-10-21.md)
qualified no clinical training labels and retained 36 null endpoint labels. The
[registry-content audit](B0%20Registry%20Content%20Feasibility%202026-10-22.md)
stopped under its frozen rule at 17/32 observable primary snapshot states
(53.125%), with no observed positive class; the later sensitivity cannot replace
that primary result. This stopped registry-content audit is distinct from the
original base-rate prediction baseline. The separate
[source-readiness route](Source%20Readiness%20Closeout%20and%20T6%20Quality%20Next%20Stage%202026-10-24.md)
was 0/12 joint-ready and also stopped. These are bounded negative feasibility
findings, not proof that every possible clinical benchmark is impossible.

Registry status/phase changes, sponsor continuation, efficacy and
approval are different endpoints. AACT availability does not resolve that
taxonomy or give adequate outcome information/power. Historical model chronology,
mapping confidence, reporting lag, censoring and dependent programs remain
requirements. Causal genetics additionally needs compatible complete regional
inputs, alleles/builds, uncertainty, units, ancestry/LD, assay identity and overlap
checks; variant associations cannot be silently converted to intervention effects.
Scoring/calibration waits for qualified labels and derivations. Downloading more
data without a named measurement does not resolve these problems. No new AACT
archive is justified by the immediate converter/T6 gate.

**Whole-inventory and release feasibility are still scoped.** This assessment
does not freshly scan every catalogue product, license or raw file. A catalogue
entry is not collected/parsed/validated data, and enough space for the measured
pilot is not a storage estimate for the entire inventory. Historical gaps cannot
be filled with current annotations. Cross-platform execution, matching-version
maintenance, independent backup and release packaging need their own evidence.
The downloader and stopped periodic monitor remain outside this workstream.

## Critical review of the next major step

### 1. Finish the correction before treating 0.3.10 as a new baseline

The duration fix is necessary, proportionate and aligned with the plan. It closes
a demonstrated metadata inconsistency without claiming an original runtime
overrun. Its targeted tests are sufficient to justify full validation, not release
acceptance. Wait for the existing full runner; do not restart it merely because
observation takes time or edit frozen source while it runs. Then require unchanged
pins/protected hashes, probes/package, a prospectively declared same-input simulated
qualification, isolated installed source/model replay/relocation and the retained
nine matching old verifiers. Preserve all failed attempts and original 0.3.9.

### 2. Use the actual pinned converter, with a meaningful negative outcome

The next major technical uncertainty is native schema enforcement, rather than
another generic controller. A standard JSON Schema engine and the AsOf
post-validator agree on the prepared 109 cases apart from the declared
cross-claim-text-uniqueness exception. That does not establish the language admitted
by llama.cpp's converter or sampler. Conversion returning a nonempty string is
also insufficient proof of conformance.

The owner-installed toolchain now passes its
[own-source x64 SDK/STL compile/link probe](review-assets/recovery-step9-native-grammar-20261028/TOOLCHAIN-LOCAL-PROBE-v2.json)
without a restart. It replaces the earlier missing-toolchain constraint, but
does not establish interoperability with the producer's Clang20.1.8 DLLs. Eight
initial and five converter-specific exact-commit reference files are retained
under capped/hash-verified acquisition; the
[three-header consumer closure](review-assets/recovery-step9-native-grammar-20261028/CONVERTER-HEADER-CLOSURE.json)
is checked. These are references/build preparation, not executed foreign code or
a native conformance result.

Follow the [build-only contract](review-assets/recovery-step9-native-grammar-20261028/CONVERTER-BRIDGE-BUILD-CONTRACT.md):
an author-written small typed bridge, exact declarations and ordinary C++ object
lifetime, approved actual DLL/import identities, release x64/standard-library
settings, constrained DLL search and isolated finite execution. Do not guess C++
object layouts or substitute a source-rebuilt converter while calling it the
installed binary. Build success alone authorizes no DLL operation. Freeze the
separate execution protocol after the framework gate passes.

First test only a fixed tiny ABI JSON round-trip and conversion of the corpus's
one shared schema, with the actual default converter mode. Capture diagnostics,
exceptions/crashes, loaded-module paths and grammar bytes/hash. **An incomplete
conversion warning, unsupported backend, exception or ABI failure closes this
declared converter gate negative and stops before vocabulary initialization.**
Do not turn a warning into a pass because a permissive grammar still parses JSON.

Two concrete risks deserve explicit negative tests. The exact-commit
[grammar documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/848bbda9041decd87904f7eb5a2990863ecce03b/grammars/README.md)
and [converter implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/848bbda9041decd87904f7eb5a2990863ecce03b/common/json-schema-to-grammar.cpp)
make unsupported `uniqueItems` handling and combined string-pattern/length
constraints relevant inspection targets. Their behavior in the actual binary is
not yet measured. The duplicated-reference and 1,800/1,801-character ASCII/Unicode
cases must remain in the fixed corpus. Standard array uniqueness is distinct
from the already declared cross-claim-text rule enforced only after generation.
An unannounced extra weakening is not covered by that exception.

### 3. Keep later recognition and inference gates separate

Only a clean converter result permits a separately declared grammar acceptance
stage. Character/EOF recognition and vocabulary-specific token/EOG filtering
prove different things. A non-null sampler may be a no-op with an empty grammar;
it is not a pass. Any later vocabulary-only path must use exact typed defaults,
verified loader behavior, CPU/device restrictions, exact byte reconstruction,
fresh grammar state per document and positive/negative completion controls.
Do not accept a rejected or incomplete EOG token merely to continue a test.

Vocabulary-only GGUF initialization is still model-related metadata loading and
must be counted that way, even if no weight tensors or inference context are
loaded. It must not be described as “no model load.” A later finite native
inference diagnostic is a third gate, with its own declaration, complete-case
timer, resource measurements and no automatic retry. Grammar success does not
revoke the old R07 stop or demonstrate better model quality.

### 4. Do not let another technical pass stand in for the human pilot

Fresh R10 generation still needs the R08 frame, source sufficiency and HR2–HR3
references first. A synthetic or explicitly exposed engineering diagnostic can
have a different, narrower authorization; its outputs cannot become fresh
performance cases. Freeze inclusion and answer requirements before seeing model
outputs, preserve source-only reference order, then measure review time, critical
errors, uncertainty and disagreement. Only that evidence can determine whether
the planned H5 study is feasible and how to size it.

## How the plan should be managed from here

The engineering work has reduced real failure risks. It has also accumulated
eight R09 slices and nine matching historical verifiers without yet producing a
qualified fresh scientific evaluation unit. Continuing indefinitely with technical
micro-milestones would miss the research objective. The next converter probe must
have a finite scope, measurable acceptance and a valid negative closeout, rather
than becoming an excuse for a new platform, larger model search or corpus repair.

At each future major completion, retain a dated reassessment of the entire
programme: current accepted versus working versions; R01–R16/HR state; what the
new evidence proves and cannot prove; scientific/data/resource gaps; and a critical
go/no-go review of the next major step. Reference the newest authoritative
artifacts and preserve earlier snapshots. Do not rerun the whole project merely
to rewrite a status document, or create another periodic monitoring task.

The immediate order remains **0.3.10 acceptance → actual-DLL converter gate →
conditional grammar acceptance/resource diagnostic**, while fresh sources and
independent human work proceed under their own prerequisites. Scientific H5 and
the original clinical/genetics/extensions remain uncompleted until their
respective evidence exists. A stopped or infeasible branch should receive an
explicit scoped disposition; it should not disappear from the project accounting.
