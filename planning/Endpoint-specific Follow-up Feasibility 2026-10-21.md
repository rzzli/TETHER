## Reconciliation and corrections

on null labels. Agreement on missing/unqualified outcomes is not an accuracy result;
no meaningful classifier performance or inter-rater discrimination follows.

The final canonical records preserve every inherited source hold and add explicit
endpoint-specific risk reasons. Inherited higher-phase-history holds are provenance,
not a blanket risk gate for efficacy. The reconciliation corrects three issues
without rewriting the sealed first-pass reports:

- A one-page consent cover's raw `7-12-2023` date has an unqualified day/month
  convention. Its inferred exact July date is not used; a consent cover is not an SAP.

  dosing risk concerns whether that exact arm was already dosed. All remain unqualified.
- Efgartigimod had two origin registered primary objectives but one reported primary
  safety composite. No authentic amendment/objective bridge was established; an
  additional hold remains. This does not promote secondary efficacy.
