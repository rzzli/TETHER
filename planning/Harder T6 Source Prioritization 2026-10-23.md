records fitting the actual shared12000-byte/max12-record packet after worst-role

download or larger prompt is permitted. Fewer eligible tasks are reported honestly.

The new task layer explicitly projects one category from a fully source-verified
case store. Parent world, raw lineage, original source coverage and task scope are
retained separately. Legacy complete-ten-case and default top-three contracts stay
unchanged. Top-one applies consistently to native output schemas, parsing, accepted
role history, simple baselines, claim commits and semantic verification.

Two fresh-context AI reviewers receive only the exact shared scientific candidate
packet, hypothesis and frozen prioritization question. They assess every candidate,
source context, uncertainty and admissible best set. Both reviews are sealed before
a third reconciler sees them; all reference artifacts are sealed before model
dispatch. Reviewers receive no previous references, model outputs or internet
enrichment. Ties and none-sufficient answers remain valid. This source-bounded test
does not need external-literature claims; such claims require a separate evaluation.

Four arms share candidates and output cap: AsOf, adapted Virtual Biotech, lexical
and provider score. Provider scores are comparable only within one identified
datasource with finite known values; otherwise that baseline is inapplicable.
No category-removal ablation is claimed for a single-category task. No explanation
quality is claimed from ID-only responses. All ties may make the challenge weak;
that is a retained finding, not permission to manufacture ranking differences.

After source-inclusive output verification and prediction sealing, report paired
task/case descriptive agreement with admissible best sets, context mismatches,
abstentions, reference disagreement/uncertainty, failures and resource use. Every
selected annotation must resolve to the original row. No inferential superiority,
clinical AUROC, therapeutic correctness or independent human-validation claim is
supported by these exposed AI-source-adjudicated tasks. Features stay empty and
probabilities null; zero provider spending and no automatic call retries.
