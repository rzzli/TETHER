# Endpoint-specific origin sample — October 21, 2026

The endpoint definitions and new origin-only development sample are frozen. This
completes the design-and-sampling phase within Step 1; clinical-label qualification,
power and training remain unresolved. Follow-up has not been opened for this sample.

## Frozen definitions

[The protocol](protocols/endpoint-origin-feasibility-v0.1.json) separates a selected
trial's primary efficacy decision, a same-program higher-phase registry start and
experimental-arm first dosing. Each requires its own origin risk set. Clinical
observation, formal analysis/decision and first public disclosure remain separate.
The old twelve cases, their protocols and all 36 unresolved labels are unchanged.

A definitive failed primary decision can supply decision polarity only when its
prospective rule, analysis, regimen/population and occurrence time qualify. A
patient-response threshold or secondary nominal p value is insufficient. Missing
results are unknown. An authentic pre-analysis protocol/SAP made public later may
be evaluator evidence after version/timing review; it cannot become an origin
predictor. No endpoint is automatically qualified by these definitions.

rows and extracted pointers checked. Eight designs are efficacy-oriented, one has
mixed efficacy/safety primaries, two have safety primaries, and one measures
physiological effects whose clinical-efficacy interpretation is unqualified. These
are readiness categories, not established biological truth or outcome labels.

The review established no complete authenticated primary efficacy decision rule
from these packets. It did find partial information that must remain visible:
hidradenitis planning includes alpha/power assumptions, and the cervical-cancer
study describes a stage-one stopping/progression rule. Neither alone establishes
the final primary-success algorithm, analysis time or a qualified outcome.

can receive a borrowed all-cohort or monotherapy label. Avelumab has mixed primary
objectives requiring the joint/multiplicity rule. Etavopivat's cerebral physiological
measurements cannot silently replace a clinical-efficacy endpoint.
