# Scientific feasibility decision

**Repository stabilization can proceed; a scientific evaluation and live model
expansion cannot yet pass their evidence gates.** The working route is a contemporary
T6 dossier-methods study addressing part of H5. This is a proposed development route,
not a claim that a biological benchmark has been validated. Clinical probabilities
remain null. H1/H2 comparisons, H3 popularity confounding, H4 causal genetics and H6
single-cell reconstruction retain their original meanings and are deferred.

A read-only filename search of the authorized E: data root on October 12 found no
files matching AACT, GTEx, FinnGen, INTERVAL, pQTL, VCF or PLINK bed/bim/fam patterns.
This is a limited discovery check, not proof that those datasets are unavailable or
absent under other names. No transport progress polling, download or account operation
was performed. The user was asked for existing approved data paths and reviewer availability;
neither is assumed. No HTTP status is being treated as a successful historical restore.

For a bounded T1 access test, use **January 1, 2023 origin / January 1, 2025 24-month
endpoint**, with a separately dated later label/publication freeze as a candidate only.
These dates are not locked or known available snapshots. If the exact origin cannot
be restored, record the gap; do not fill it with current records. Changing the endpoint
or selecting a different date requires a new protocol before inspecting outcome rates.
Contemporary October 2026 predictions with 24-month follow-up mature in October 2028
before reporting lag. A June 2027 deliverable can concern methods, not those outcomes.

## T6 development review protocol, ready for reviewer input

The unit is one target–indication–action–modality–tissue hypothesis. Reused evidence
and repeated targets are dependence clusters; the ten development cases are not ten
independent clinical outcomes and must not become a held-out generalization set.

Working estimand: the paired case-level change in source-grounded dossier utility
between the offline extractor and a future bounded specialist, using identical frozen
evidence and acquisition coverage. Report unsupported-claim rate, source/method errors,
coverage of a reviewer-defined relevant evidence set, major omissions, context errors,
latency and measured cost separately. A combined score needs prespecified weights and
reviewer agreement; none are invented here.

Each reviewer record should include case ID and hypothesis fingerprint; anonymized
system ID; source/row locator; claim text; source support (supported/contradicted/not
assessable); action/modality relevance; context/method correctness; error severity;
missingness handling; relevant omitted evidence; rationale and adjudication status.
Use `not assessable` for unavailable information rather than treating missing evidence
as negative biology. Check opposite-action and modality reuse explicitly.

Two independent domain reviewers are the preferred design. Confirm their availability,
conflicts and time allocation; blind system labels and randomize paired presentation.
Retain disagreements before adjudication. Agree on an error-severity rubric, a minimum
useful improvement, maximum unsupported-claim tolerance and stopping criteria before
seeing comparative results. If only one reviewer is available, label the work a
single-reviewer development screen and do not claim independent consensus gold.

Store filled gold records under ignored evaluator-only benchmark assets, with catalog
roles and lineage preventing agent access. Labeled synthetic conflict fixtures test
software behavior; they do not count as observed biological conflicts or expert gold.
Reserve new cases and target clusters before any locked evaluation. Estimate paired
variability and review burden from development cases before selecting evaluation size.

## Decision boundary

Continue offline maintenance and prepare the review package. Do not start a live
adapter experiment until the T6 protocol, reviewer capacity, eligible data and an
explicit spending decision exist. Do not start T1 scorer training until restored
historical data, actual event counts, chronology and attainable follow-up pass review.
This is an explicit incomplete feasibility gate, not a negative finding about the
scientific value of the approach. No power estimate, clinical cohort, regional QC
result or independent adjudication was fabricated to close it.
