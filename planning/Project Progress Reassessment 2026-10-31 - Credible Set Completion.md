## Critical next step

First close P04 with complete attempt accounting, actual permitted arms if the
host view qualifies, source-inclusive semantic checks and measured resources.
Two exposed contexts plus one synthetic withholding condition are a development
pilot, not three independent cases or a clinical benchmark. Literal projection
is the ceiling comparator and must remain visible even if models perform worse.

Then implement a separately versioned P05 source-only builder. The current P04
builder intentionally fixes two targets, Europe PMC records and identity/date/
cell-type fields. The generic compiler already supports exact joins and native
measurement fields; a new neutral selector must build directly from eligible
originals, independently of evaluator-only selected-row packets.

Before any fresh semantic scan, freeze the candidate universe, deterministic
selection/exclusion rules, source/receipt/schema pins, eligibility and rights,
exposure categories, missingness strata, and article/study/cohort/locus/family
dependence rules. Reconcile the catalog's stale queued credible-set metadata
against retained acquisition evidence. Do not equate a native study ID with
independently verified study/cohort identity. Unknown dependence needs an explicit
worst-case sensitivity or an exploratory claim, not invented independent units.

The proposed next task is native measurement composition with original source
bindings and explicit unknown units/sample size/ancestry. Freeze its measurement
groups separately from identity fields and the P04 endpoint. Actual eligible
counts, cluster support, precision and compute fit must determine the final
protocol. A small exploratory cap is reasonable; twelve qualified groups or a
particular effect size are not yet established. Source-only agents may assist
reference construction and checking, but they supply no external expert validation.
