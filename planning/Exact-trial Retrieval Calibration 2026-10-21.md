## Frozen procedure and implementation

and implementation were committed and pushed at `712c4fb` before dispatch. Two
independent AI agents reviewed the immutable controls, parent hashes, criteria
and scientific boundaries before execution. Their reports and original failure
probes are retained under `data/benchmark/labels/source-readiness-20261021/`.

`tools/science/source_retrieval.py` adds evaluator-only exact-NCT result triage,
explicit projection of registry document references and one-attempt original
transport. Query labels cannot supply a result's identity. An exact NCT mention
qualifies only a candidate; background tables and unrelated arms remain
unverified. The structured route checks the resource's own NCT and projects
document metadata without results or outcomes. Nested metadata objects are
rejected rather than copied into the projection.

Transport reserves each output directory before dispatch, preserves a hash-chained
journal, checks frozen HTTPS hosts and bounded redirects, caps each body at16MiB,
retains original bytes and sanitized headers, and compares the frozen SHA and PDF
signature. Errors/interruption retain failure records or partial bodies. No
automatic retries or output reuse occur. The120-second soft elapsed budget is
checked between reads/redirects; a single in-flight I/O can overrun it by the
40-second I/O timeout. This is not hard real-time cancellation.

Nineteen new regressions include independent adversarial probes. All635 full
tests passed; the dependency-free suite also passed with57 expected ingestion
skips. Required verification probes, PowerShell parsing, wheel packaging and the
