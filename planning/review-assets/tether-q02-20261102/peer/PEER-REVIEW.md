# Independent Q02 catalog and binding review

Reviewed the design, all 38 proposed cases, the pure artifact-policy implementation, the sealed binding/CLI integration and the neutral metadata importer. This is a local Windows engineering review of TETHER 0.5.0 over the unchanged AsOf 0.3.11 engine. It does not run the real ten-case frame, rehash provider originals, open clinical outcomes or human responses, or rerun a paper experiment. Generated fixtures are explicitly fictional and retained under ignored `data/cache/tether-q02-peer/`.

## Final result

**66 assertions pass:** 32 pure-policy checks and 34 binding/execution checks. These are related assertions over constructed fixtures, not independent scientific observations or the platform's separate test-suite count. Their source hashes match the final reviewed runtime. Installed-wheel qualification and the actual metadata-import audit belong to the platform/data reviewers' separate records.

The positive fixture contains an old eligible raw input, a nested mapping computed after the cutoff using that input, and a nested normalized packet. Its contemporary-profile T6 case has no scorer. During one genuine offline run, every original case/snapshot/policy/catalog/roots control, the original normalized packet/mapping and the original metadata evidence file are overwritten after preparation. The engine executes the exact copied layout and successfully verifies the retained policy, source and deterministic dossier relationships. The raw original and its pinned receipt remain available as explicitly required by the legacy engine.

The negative checks reject optional Virtual Biotech/reference relabeling, actual QC/outcome ancestry, scorer input, an unused evaluator node, missing parents, cycles, unregistered same-product artifacts, unreviewed selected requirements, future raw annotation, substituted availability and unqualified origin/evaluator/manual-archive uses. Recomputing a caller-visible projection does not reuse mutated returned grants. A changed legacy evaluator role with a recomputed structural fingerprint still fails against the actual original catalog bytes.

At the wrapper boundary, wrong frame, missing/changed provenance evidence, changed receipt, unsupported synthetic profile and scorer flags fail with **zero engine calls**, retained failure records and no success receipt. Rehashed binding records cannot grant an optional evaluator product through a generic projection, omit policy controls or transplant a different frame. Existing schema-one projection shapes remain readable by the legacy reader; passing schema two directly to that reader is rejected. This does not retroactively add artifact-level protection to direct legacy APIs.

A separate copied-packet fixture makes every observation future-published. The existing temporal admission excludes all those records before backend use: zero backend calls and an empty ledger. Its artifact-policy consistency remains verified. This is record exclusion, not a false claim that the whole input policy failed or that no evidence exists biologically.

## Boundaries that remain

The schema-one projection grants product roles and is not a standalone artifact authorization mechanism. The stronger contract requires the canonical schema-two binding: exact policy/use/frame/case/snapshot and all supplied source identities/actual parent edges. Optional dependency descriptions and validation attestations never replace real source ancestry. Trained-scorer inference, replay and local-model execution remain unsupported on this path; Q02 does not implement their future training chronology contracts.

The selected frame hash is an externally supplied identity anchor; its meaning is not discovered from the digest alone. Retained proof bytes establish equality to declared anchors, not publisher authenticity, complete real-world ancestry, scientific adequacy or independent adjudication. Source acquisition, parsed subsets and scientific validation remain separate states. All scientific-validation fields remain unestablished.

Copied controls prevent reopening unchecked mutable originals. This is not filesystem isolation against a hostile process rewriting reserved outputs. External raw payloads and their original pinned receipts remain required; changing a receipt after preparation fails rather than silently following new transport metadata. No receipt was rewritten to pass. Availability and publication checks remain additional to catalog roles; computation time alone does not make old information ineligible.

The metadata importer was reviewed without reading biological rows. Its use of actual July 2, 2023 dump metadata and blocked AACT origin/follow-up uses is appropriate. The actual imported counts, source evidence pins and final seven AACT records are independently audited by the data reviewer, not claimed as freshly reproduced by these fictional tests. The prior catalog, old packets/protocols and frozen scientific results remain unchanged.
