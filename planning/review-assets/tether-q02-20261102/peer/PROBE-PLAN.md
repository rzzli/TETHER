# Independent Q02 admission and binding probes

This reviewer has read the schema-2 proposal and all 38 proposed acceptance cases.
The planned probes below are not passing evidence until executed against the
implemented interface. Test inputs are fictional or existing synthetic demo files.
No original study, biological-provider row, clinical outcome or human response is
opened. Generated trees belong under ignored `data/cache/tether-q02-peer/`; compact
scripts, findings and result records belong here.

## Primary counterexamples

1. An unselected evaluator node is present in the supplied snapshot. It must be
   denied even if no accepted claim references it.
2. A permitted derived product actually consumes evaluator/QC or optional Virtual
   Biotech label bytes. Calling that product edge a validation dependency or
   assigning the source a reference role must not exempt the actual ancestor.
3. A trained scorer consumes outcome/split labels. The source DAG remains real
   training ancestry; schema-2 evidence admission must not authorize it by
   reclassification or by moving its control file after preflight.
4. Origin and follow-up AACT files share a product. An endpoint artifact cannot
   become origin context by changing only a request string or candidate cutoff.
   Qualified uses require exact frame, protocol, origin and artifact identity.
5. A source hash/path/size is correct but its receipt ID, URL, release or path case
   differs. Identity comparison must reject it before any child/backend launch.
6. A normalized packet is nested below the snapshot. Its copied snapshot must
   resolve exactly the copied relative layout, with raw and derived source nodes
   preserving the appropriate base-root semantics.
7. Original mutable controls change after validation. Execution must use the
   sealed inspected copies or fail; it must not reopen changed original paths.
8. A projection is copied from another valid use/case/frame. A legacy schema-1
   reader may accept its shape; the canonical binding must reject the transplant.
9. A nonselected artifact shares a product with an allowed artifact. Product-level
   permissions in the projection must not grant canonical access to the new bytes.
10. A policy file or policy binding is omitted from an otherwise internally valid
    run, then the envelope is rehashed. Stronger verification must reject it.

## Positive controls and preserved boundaries

Use one complete fictional eligible DAG with a nested normalized packet to prove
that role gates and copying do not reject every case. Show that the generated
projection is deterministic, leaves nonselected products denied and contains no
automatic optional inputs. Keep policy qualification separate from execution
completion and scientific qualification. Exercise a failed inner run after valid
preflight and verify inspection does not redispatch.

Schema 1 remains compatible under its prior guarantees. Direct legacy readers must
reject schema 2. No old artifact acquires a new policy guarantee, and no old P05
policy, packet, protocol or result is replaced. Policy denial before engine launch
should show zero engine/backend calls; record-level temporal rejection may launch
the engine but must still precede backend invocation. Report those separately.

## Timing and ownership limits

Stable reads and copied inputs prevent reopening an unchecked mutable original.
They are not operating-system protection against a hostile process rewriting a
reserved output after ownership checks. The test threat model and final capability
table must state this limit. A supplied manifest cannot disclose undeclared
real-world ancestry; consistency of its declared complete DAG is the tested claim.
