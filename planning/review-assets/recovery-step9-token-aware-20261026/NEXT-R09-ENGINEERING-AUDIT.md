## Reuse instead of a new controller

- `chrono.EvidenceStore.query` and `InputGraph` supply exact case/category/program/temporal/role admission. Use the current authority/root/receipt locks from `study.source_preparation`; do not expose raw paths or filesystem access to an agent.
- `agents.make_request` and `retrieval.bounded_request` already implement deterministic whole-record selection and an exclusion trace. `verify_retrieval_trace` rebuilds that selection from an admitted store. Its ranking is literal **case-JSON** overlap; it is not question relevance or evidence strength. Its EvidenceView may annotate feature applicability, so selected IDs must be resolved back to the original GroundedObservations for compilation, never copied from altered view serialization.
- `study.source_rows.audit_selected` reconstructs complete method2.2 records and mapping rows. For retrieval, audit **all candidate fields/records that influence ranking**, before ranking. Auditing only the selected output permits a corrupted normalized candidate statement to change who wins despite correct raw file hashes. An explicitly bounded candidate universe avoids demanding a full release replay.
- `study.source_preparation.compile_request` preserves original selected records, citations, coverage and grounded hashes; `diagnostic_codec.bind_question` supplies the existing interpretation contract. `preparation_payloads.prepare_payloads` prepares source-complete role payloads without generation. Tokenization quotes exact finalized payloads; it cannot be silently plugged into unchanged StudySession byte capacity.
- `StudySession`/RecordedBackend/explicit StudyVerifier already provide one-attempt accounting and immutable replay. Use them for a later invented retrieved-input fixture composition. The fixed_evidence public fixture gate stays intact; a distinct versioned verifier must authenticate a retrieval receipt and selected-source identity. Do not clone session/process/accounting implementations or monkeypatch the fixture allowlist.
- `tools/science/comparison_workflow.py` demonstrates category-bounded preparation and role adaptation but is repository-specific selection code, with additional unneeded arms and native assumptions. It is reference material, not an installed import/dependency for this new track.
