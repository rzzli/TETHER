cannot qualify the actual DLL, vocabulary EOG, full-vocabulary masking cost or
native prefix-stack behavior. First close exact public-header/loader prerequisites,

margin excludes real startup/inference. Do not enlarge budgets, weaken admission,
substitute an easier case or repeat until a favorable time appears.
