## Findings that must survive final closure

1. **The natural-task result is negative for incremental semantic utility.** SciFact retains 27/300 strict-correct predictions, while LitQA2 retains 12/199 correct answers. Every candidate passes its scoped provenance gate. The 273 SciFact strict failures include evidence/rationale errors, and LitQA2's 187 incorrect-and-admitted outcomes include 178 unsure responses. Neither count is a count of that many substantive false factual assertions. These completed measurements do not validate scientific reasoning or clinical utility.

2. **The paired scientific arms test filtering of fixed candidates.** They share the producer and source pipeline. Filtering cannot increase correct answers per all scheduled cases without generating or repairing an answer; it can alter error admission, coverage or selective precision. These runs admit everything, so even that tradeoff was not demonstrated. This is a limitation of the tested intervention, not a reason to relabel a completed run as missing.

3. **Inspect archive interoperability passed; full production runtime equivalence did not.** The same frozen 109-case policy suite conforms in all 981 repeated cells. Separately, 19 deliberately constructed runtime dispositions passed independent reconstruction, including expected failure and nonexecution. Full specialist scheduling, positive review retry, live schema-two facade behavior, generic feature reconstruction and a persisted native write-fault control remain outside the port. The plan explicitly requires honest reporting of missing parity; it does not require falsely treating those absent paths as passed before this experiment can close. The final report preserves that distinction.

4. **A reused independent validator is substantial code, not a thin convenience dependency.** Its eight modules and 2,642 physical lines must remain counted. The native runtime reader separately checks source/claim identities and five constrained synthetic feature/scoring states; it does not independently implement all biological eligibility rules. The shared TETHER process supervisor must also remain disclosed. Neither this stack nor trusted shared-process reference isolation is an adversarial sandbox.

5. **Timing and test scopes cannot be pooled.** BG02's cold per-case TETHER/independent ratio of 1.65 and BG05's warm-within-batch ratio of 0.9443 describe different conditions. They do not isolate a causal import optimization or universal speed advantage. BG05's matched Inspect/TETHER ratio is 1.5465. LitQA2's missing marginal-check and full monotonic generation-stage measurements remain unknown. Release, facade, component and reconstruction test counts overlap and refer to different pinned versions; they are not additive or scientific sample sizes.

6. **Chronology and protocol amendments are part of the result.** BG01 qualifies 0.5.1/0.3.12; BG02 exposes an additional replay defect and separately qualifies 0.5.2/0.3.13. Its arms share identical inputs within each version, while cross-version archives are matched semantic twins. LitQA2's failed fictional semantic control remains failed; prospective amendment A01 permits a weak-baseline measurement, and A02 fixes worker cache paths before inference. Neither amendment upgrades the failed capability control or permits retrospective retuning. Current acceptance02 concerns mutable-document text repair, with unchanged experiment seals.

7. **Public data and selected source access limit interpretation.** SciFact uses public development claims and separately authorized fit supervision; held-out references remain excluded during inference. LitQA2 uses all original questions but a retrieval-assisted condition distinct from the original tool-free experiment. Only ten of 199 questions have an observed annotated-source DOI hit under the declared route. Rights, public exposure, source identity, decisive-passage access and answer entailment are separate properties. None can be inferred from the others.
