## Completion judgment

The plan allows weak task results and expressly requires reporting missing Inspect parity. It does not require SOTA accuracy, proof of biological truth, positive runtime portability in every unported path, or a successful full product migration. Therefore the completed 300-claim SciFact and 199-question LitQA2 measurements, and the finite archive-parity/partial-runtime Inspect experiment, satisfy their execution requirements while remaining negative or limited capability findings.

The final audit must retain that distinction. It must not convert the 109 authored policy cases into 981 independent samples, convert an admitted wrong answer into a scientific success, or convert a source receipt into physical execution/knowledge provenance.
