## Metric and denominator safeguards

- **Matched candidates:** admission of fixed candidates cannot increase correct-and-admitted/all scheduled beyond raw-correct/all scheduled. The SciFact and LitQA2 checks preserved the correct outputs and admitted the observed source-valid errors. Their equality demonstrates no semantic accuracy advantage. Invalid-source control rejection is a different outcome from answering correctly.
- **SciFact:** the strict complete-case criterion is supplemental to four official micro-aggregated metric families. Of 27 strict-correct cases, 26 are NEI and one is evidence-bearing. Retrieval covers 159/209 annotated document pairs and the full gold document set for 147/188 evidence-bearing claims. Poor strict performance can reflect extra documents, wrong labels and incomplete rationales; it does not isolate biological misunderstanding. Constant NEI's 112/300 is post hoc arithmetic, not an executed arm or evidence-finding success.
- **LitQA2:** 12/199 accuracy, 12/21 precision and 21/199 coverage have different denominators. The 178 unsure responses are valid task outputs, not 178 operational failures or substantive false assertions. The original-string versus strict-JSON malformed-response convention is disclosed, even though the actual run had zero parse failures. The 24.43% random-substantive expectation is an unexecuted, different-coverage reference; it does not supply a matched significance test.
- **Source alignment:** 10/205 DOI-pair and 10/199 question-hit counts are identifier diagnostics. The 10/16 conditional resolved subset is selected through retrieved metadata and cannot replace the all-annotation denominator. Decisive-passage inclusion and entailment remain unmeasured. The six-correct-with-hit versus six-correct-without-hit association is post hoc and noncausal.
- **Sampling:** 1,308 or 981 cells are repeated exposed constructions, not independent natural failures. The 19 runtime dispositions include intended errors, an unresolved interruption and an unexecuted follow-on case. Source-connected public claims and questions are also not guaranteed independent; no new population-power or confidence claim is warranted.

## Resources and efficiency

The cost reports preserve the relevant distinctions. BG02's median paired **TETHER/independent ≈ 1.65** uses fresh processes per case; BG05's **0.9443** uses fresh serial-batch processes with different wrappers. BG05's **Inspect/TETHER = 1.5465** is descriptive, not a causal claim about one import or hashing mechanism. Line inventories include the 2,642 reused policy lines and 1,010 integration lines, while full-runtime gaps prevent equal-feature maintenance claims.

SciFact's 587.287-second reported workflow includes 486.680 seconds of repeated shared verification. Its direct check time and attributable checking brackets are separately reported; the whole residual cannot be assigned as marginal admission cost. LitQA2's 1,161.953-second supervised worker excludes uninstrumented parent finalization, and marginal checking cost is unavailable. Its 882.172-second evaluator and 17.203-second native reconstruction perform different obligations. The 512 MiB allocation microprobe is a concrete inefficiency, not an exact decomposition of production latency. Bytes are logical/product measures rather than physical I/O; memory peaks are not additive; $0 provider spending excludes local hardware, energy and labour cost.
