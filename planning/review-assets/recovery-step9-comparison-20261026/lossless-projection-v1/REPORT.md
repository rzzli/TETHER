## Disposition

The experiment stops here as prospectively required. There was one representation, one guide policy, and one measurement per fixed input; there was no search over source omissions or representations. The real-format limit is not mainly duplicate text or removable wrapper scaffolding. A generic source-preserving controller implementation cannot make this six-full-source-stage design fit under the measured fixed bound.

The next gate is an explicit study-design decision: retain the negative result under the current requirements, or prospectively authorize a changed schedule/representation and its scientific retention criteria. That is a change to the comparison specification, not permission to silently increase budgets, omit scientific fields, amputate roles, replace sources, or run a native trial. A relevant scientific review of any changed representation would remain separate from byte equality. Original Virtual Biotech end-to-end retrieval and tool contracts remain unreproduced.
