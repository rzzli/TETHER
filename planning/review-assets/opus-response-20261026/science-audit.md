# Independent scientific feasibility audit for the Opus response

Prepared October 26, 2026 UTC. Scope: original hypothesis/protocol documents, current project memory/checklist, retained aggregate scientific qualification reports, and failed-pilot adjudication. Read-only production review; no model calls, acquisition, clinical outcome-file access, or source edits. This is an AI methods assessment, not independent human validation. Statements below distinguish retained observations from proposed design choices.

## Bottom line

The project has a credible auditability/engineering artifact and multiple useful negative feasibility findings. It does not yet establish independent dossier quality, clinical prediction, calibrated probabilities, causal genetics, or superiority to the released Virtual Biotech. The shortest defensible research route is a clearly amended contemporary T6/H5 study, with a small diagnostic study before committing to a larger blinded human evaluation. H1–H4 remain conditional, not disproved and not permanently impossible. H6 is a separate data-dependent question.

The current interpretation failure combines interface/instruction ambiguity and model noncompliance with a source-specific usefulness failure. Available source context was omitted. A stronger model could diagnose capability, but one changed-model attempt does not isolate capacity from task/interface differences and cannot qualify general dossier quality.

## Evidence anchors and qualifications to Opus

1. **H5 changed sample size is a new scope.** Original proposal H5 is preference plus lower error and lower cost, with six scientists rating 40 pairs; T6 separately proposes about 50 pairs (`planning/Beyond the Virtual Biotech Tooling Gaps and Project Plan.md:119,145,199`). Opus's two experts/20 cases can be a pilot amendment, not completion of that original protocol. Neither 20 cases nor two reviewers guarantees adequate power or sufficient clinical-domain coverage.
2. **Clinical data unavailable is not population infeasibility.** The fixed purposive Science 4 audit comprises 12 programs × three endpoints, all unresolved. It does not establish zero decisive clinical outcomes across the registry or zero historical training inventory. `item6/training-feasibility-r1.json` explicitly records inventory size as unknown, not zero. Missing reporting is not failure, a new phase registration/dosing is not efficacy, and sponsor discontinuation is not automatically biological failure.
3. **The registry-content B0 route is not the original B0 predictor.** Current January coverage 17/32 = 53.125% refers to the registry-content feasibility branch (`Remaining Scientific Gates...:11`). Original baseline B0 is a base-rate predictor by phase/area/modality. Use distinct names in the new plan to avoid implying a base-rate model failed with that coverage rate.
4. **Genetics is technically advanced but scientifically unqualified.** The retained full-cis data exist: 38 files, 93,166,905 bytes, 2,979 variants in the LD-QC subset; Science 5 rechecked 4,096 LD entries to approximately 3.4e-15 agreement. There is also a distinct 3,968 shared-statistic inventory; an LD-filtered set cannot silently become the dense colocalization set. This is BLUEPRINT naive-monocyte transcript abundance and RA susceptibility, not circulating IL6R protein, pharmacologic inhibition, or treatment response. Ancestry/scale/overlap, source digest discrepancy, method-specific input coverage and exact historical lineage remain held (`item5/genetics-disposition.json`). Do not restart all acquisition as though no regional data exist.
5. **Genetics is not a prerequisite for every clinical scorer.** The retained Science 6 guard explicitly permits a non-genetic route in principle. It remains blocked by unqualified labels, biological predictors, splits, dependence, adequacy and calibration design. Finish label/predictor feasibility before making genetics the critical path for a basic clinical benchmark.
6. **Historical and contemporary model routes must remain separate.** The current Qwen weights are immutable and predate the proposed January 2025 origin, but their exact training cutoff is unknown (`Evaluation Chronology...:11–18`). A new 2026 capability-ceiling model may be useful for contemporary T6, but cannot be inserted into the January 2025 historical clinical arms without changing the estimand and contamination claim. Model release chronology is evidence, not proof of absent training contamination.
7. **MR interpretation did not fail on the latest run.** The first mechanism task failed and the MR task was not dispatched (`Question Pilot Failure Closeout...:18,39`). Its performance is unknown; do not count an unrun task as a scientific error or successful abstention. Schema validity, source fidelity, citation entailment and positive scientific utility are separate dimensions.
8. **The source-data gap is not the immediate mechanism failure.** The current failed IL6R source already contains useful biological context (`Question Pilot Failure Closeout...:42–45`). More AACT archives or regional genetics will not fix that omission. One source's repeated snippets are not independent studies or independent evaluation cases.
9. **Human review does not certify truth by vote.** The human panel can provide independent source-based judgments and domain interpretation, with disclosed expertise/uncertainty. It cannot create missing primary evidence, demonstrate clinical efficacy, or turn animal/cell/model inference into human response. Count independence of reviewers, studies and case components separately.
10. **Full infrastructure consolidation is not the next scientific experiment.** It is sensible engineering but should not block source-only human review, a no-spend capability diagnostic, or protocol amendment. Preserve old verifier compatibility and demonstrate restoration before moving/deleting code. Only extract common runtime facilities needed by the next study initially.

## Recommended ordered scientific path

### S0. Freeze the claim map and exposure map

Create a compact current-state table covering each H1–H6/T1–T6, original meaning, evidence attained, unresolved prerequisite and allowed claim. Inventory exposed targets, questions, sources, candidate outputs and AI references separately from held-out candidate cases. IL6R and the existing ten contemporary cases remain development material. Preserve prior negative findings and their denominators.

Acceptance: every proposed next result maps to a prespecified claim; no clinical-probability or superiority wording appears without its missing prerequisites; each proposed case has project-exposure metadata. Stop if the proposed evaluation depends on treating known answers as held-out.

### S1. Amend the current phase to a bounded contemporary T6/H5 methods study

Recommend auditable source-grounded dossier quality as this phase's primary question. Keep clinical efficacy, scorer and causal genetics as explicitly deferred branches. Name adapted Virtual Biotech selection/role workflow accurately; do not call it unchanged reproduction. State whether the current interpretation layer evaluates source-conditioned analysis or full end-to-end discovery. They require different denominators and comparators.

Acceptance: one signed/dated owner decision record with primary outcome, unit, arm definitions, source world, cost boundary, development/confirmatory tiers and deferred claims. A plan is not public preregistration. Budget remains $0 until the owner explicitly authorizes otherwise.

### S2. Validate the human instrument and recruit role-matched reviewers

Separate a shareable reviewer packet from coordinator materials and prior AI judgments. First obtain source-only required findings and evidence limitations from reviewers who have not seen generated outputs, then conduct blinded candidate/paired dossier review. Use neutral A/B labels, balanced order and equal formatting/length rules. Keep calibration feedback off the actual evaluation form.

Use multiple choice for source support, context transfer, certainty, citation correctness and major omitted information. Allow 'not assessable from provided evidence', 'outside my expertise', ties and uncertainty. Provide concise source cards with exact passages, primary/review source type and context; do not require the expert to reproduce internet-scale research. Define optional bounded external-source checks as a separate layer and log them.

Two independent reviewers plus a separately assigned adjudicator is a practical pilot design, not a substitute for the original six-scientist panel. Reviewer qualifications should match actual task strata (e.g. target biology/clinical area; genetics when an MR-specific task is included). A clinician alone need not be expert in QTL assay artifacts, and a computational geneticist alone need not adjudicate ADC transfer.

Acceptance: two reviewers independently complete a short calibration packet without instructions from each other; a coordinator records time, skipped/out-of-expertise items, rubric ambiguity and disagreements. Revise ambiguous items before a new freeze. Do not set an arbitrary reviewer pass/fail percentage or use AI agreement as the competence answer key. No claim of independence if reviewers saw AI verdicts or model identity.

### S3. Diagnose contract/task/model limits on development data

Use a small finite diagnostic matrix, not another endless one-case prompt-repair loop. Hold source facts, question semantics and scoring rubric fixed while separating (i) quotation/pointer extraction and schema coherence, (ii) substantive source-conditioned interpretation, and (iii) optional stronger-model condition. If a provider supports a different schema, log the unavoidable adapter difference and retain a matched text-semantics control. Include an extractive/manual scripted positive control to establish that the task is answerable, but never count its performance as model performance.

First use existing local assets and a simpler supported wire representation to obtain feasibility information. A stronger local or API condition is optional and separately authorized; no spending estimate is a permission. A single strong-model pass/fail is a diagnostic observation only. If a stronger condition fails, this does not prove all models fail; if it succeeds, the original confounded mechanism is not identified without matched controls.

Track attempted/finalized requests, contract-valid outputs, source-citation correctness, useful context captured, unsupported transfer, abstention and latency. Retain malformed responses and charge failed calls appropriately. Same-model architecture comparisons must use the same chosen model across all arms; a model-capacity experiment is a distinct factor.

Acceptance: finite matrix and maximum calls fixed before execution, all attempts accounted for, successful source-specific positive-utility behavior on more than the known IL6R record, no undisclosed response repair. Stop the version at its predefined call ceiling; do not select the best retry as if it were a first attempt.

### S4. Prepare a fresh scientific feasibility pilot

Choose fresh project-unexposed cases using target/indication/action/modality/tissue and evidence-family strata, not known model success. Include relevant conflicting/insufficient sources when genuinely present; do not fabricate conflicts. Seal the source universe and source-only reviewer expectations before generating candidate outputs. 'Fresh to project' does not mean absent from model pretraining. Keep evaluator gold and model-visible inputs separated by capabilities, not just folder names.

At first evaluate interpretation on the same candidate evidence across arms. Separately evaluate retrieval over a fixed eligible universe to avoid conflating lexical prefilter quality with model synthesis. The original selection-only system and an extractive baseline are essential controls; an output that copies everything cannot win completeness without a cost/length consequence.

Acceptance: every case has canonical identifiers, action/modality/context, source completeness qualifiers and traceable packets; source-only human review is sealed before any output review; no replacements chosen based on observed outcomes. Pilot size is a resource/measurement design choice stated prospectively, not evidence of adequate H5 power.

### S5. Use pilot data to size the independent H5 evaluation

Predeclare source-supported claim accuracy/error, severity, omission/required-findings coverage, utility, calibration of wording, and panel preference. Include all attempted outputs in operational success and coverage denominators. Claim-level error rates must not hide all-abstention, non-finalization, duplicate claims or verbose output. Source support is distinct from underlying study validity and clinical transfer.

Plan analysis at the case/component level with repeated reviewer measurements accounted for. Use paired data for systems evaluated on the same cases. Predefine ties, abstentions, adjudication, missing reviews, multiple arms and secondary metrics. A naive sign test over every reviewer rating or every claim would pseudoreplicate correlated observations. Simulate power or expected confidence-interval width using pilot effect/variance and sensitivity scenarios; preserve original H5 targets or state the amended precision objective. Do not borrow H1's AUROC 0.05 target for H5 preference.

Acceptance: a statistician/methods-qualified review or transparent independently checked simulation supports a feasible sample allocation; the document explicitly states which original panel-size commitment changes. No adequate-power claim from arbitrary ≥20 cases alone.

### S6. Lock and conduct H5; publish bounded conclusions

Freeze code, data, case exposure checks, prompts, models, seeds, arm budgets, rubric, allocation and analysis before inference/reference joins. Generate and seal outputs before blinded evaluation; adjudicate independently without changing candidates. Keep untouched evaluation cases separate from instrument training. Report relative error, useful coverage, preference, uncertainty and costs with confidence intervals and all exclusions/failures.

Cost accounting must distinguish $0 provider bills from inference compute/wall time, research-agent usage outside the meter, expert annotation and acquisition. Match the declared cost boundary across arms. A cost reduction without scientific usefulness is not full H5 success. A negative H5 result is reportable without claiming the whole project was completed.

Acceptance: the registered/amended analysis runs once, every attempted case has a final disposition, source-inclusive verification passes, reviewer independence and study exposure are disclosed, all prespecified metrics including null results are retained. Scope findings to source-grounded dossier quality, not clinical benefit.

## Conditional branches after the bounded H5 path

### C1. Clinical T1/H1–H3, scoring and chronology

Before new cohort extraction, conduct a finite source-readiness assessment of a genuinely adjudicable outcome product. Candidate released CTO/Virtual Biotech datasets require product-specific license, endpoint semantics, positive/negative/censoring definitions, mappings, date availability and contamination/dependence review; a published label file is not automatically a qualified target-level efficacy endpoint. Do not re-open stopped registry branches by resampling replacements. Keep sponsor decision, registration, dosing and efficacy distinct.

Qualify origin-only predictors and historical annotation lineage; four historical mapping products do not constitute a predictor bundle. Plan train/calibration/test origins with grouped target/drug/indication/sponsor leakage controls. Measure qualified classes, dependency components and missingness before cohort-scale power/precision claims. The original H1 plan specifies 80% power at an AUROC difference of 0.05; use realistic paired/dependent simulations and a separate calibration-precision analysis. Do not set all unresolved labels negative or use matched pairs/repeated arms to manufacture independent N.

Only after label/predictor/split/adequacy qualification may a non-genetic scorer be trained and independently calibrated; causal-genetics remains optional for this baseline. Keep test predictions and labels untouched until the locked analysis. A stronger contemporary model cannot be backdated into historical origin. If chronology/labels/power fail, retain a documented defer rather than announcing impossibility from one purposive audit.

### C2. Genetics H4

Start from retained full-region assets and source exceptions. Specify estimator-specific complete inputs and analysis sets (including separate dense colocalization versus LD-filtered MR sets), exposure identity and scale, tissue/cell context, build/alleles, ancestry compatibility, participant overlap and historical lineage. Independently numerically validate method implementation with known-answer simulations and qualified real examples before emitting predictive features. Colocalization and MR do not by themselves establish drug action, tissue delivery, dose or clinical response. An inconclusive result remains inconclusive; weak variants and null findings are not a drug-failure label.

### C3. T2–T5 and H6

T2 needs qualified matched outcomes and component-aware inference. T3 needs a defined all-gene universe, entrant ascertainment and a positive-unlabeled interpretation; untried genes are not failures. T4 needs action/modality/tissue truth, polypharmacology handling and unresolved classes; drug mechanism and variant annotation are not sufficient therapeutic direction labels. T5 needs safety-stop causal/scope adjudication, class-specific event sufficiency, competing risks and allowed terminology; absence of a report is not safety. H6 needs the original trial/drug-level matrix, exact estimand, expression/covariates, mapping and adjusted reanalysis protocol. It is independent of a T1 performance win but not independent of its own unavailable data.

## Human participation is a separate workstream, not a single magic gate

Immediate: recruit/assign independent H5 reviewers and optionally an independent methods reviewer; agents can prepare packets and analyze returned ratings. Later conditional work may require clinical endpoint adjudication or a genetics methods specialist if claims extend there. None of those roles can fill missing primary evidence by opinion. User actions are recruitment/permissions/files/budget decisions; research assistants should complete all preparation, instrument checks, source curation and logistics templates autonomously before handing off.

## Review limitations

This pass did not re-run scientific experiments, re-open outcome labels, establish underlying primary-study validity, access provider accounts, or prove general model incapacity. It inspected retained aggregate reports and documented protocols. Proposed sample sizes, budgets and timelines should remain estimates until instrument timings, model diagnostics and pilot variance are measured. Opus's publication-feasibility judgment is reasonable strategic advice, not evidence that a publishable superiority result is guaranteed.
