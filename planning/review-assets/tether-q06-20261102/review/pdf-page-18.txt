FULL requires G0-G4. WITHOUT_WRAPPER requires G0,G1,G3,G4. WIRE_ONLY requires G0 and G4's
schema status, not G4's exactness status. An unexecuted guard is not automatically a pass. F02 and F09 may
still be admitted by WIRE_ONLY because their required schema/byte checks pass while stronger source
checks do not. No policy is credited with factual source fidelity solely because the wire is valid. All intended
decisions remain in the ledger, including early guard failures.
There are 24 native outputs: twelve literal plus twelve abstentions. Literal outputs have 36 decisions and
abstentions twelve. Fourteen controls have 42 decisions. Total: 38 outputs and 90 decisions. All 90 reach a
terminal verdict; 57 admit and 33 reject under their respective policies. Returned36 and deliberately
malformed2 is output accounting, not decision accounting. The 35 core-scored-output subset is secondary.
These denominators must not be mixed.
