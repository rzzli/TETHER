Internal hashes alone cannot detect wholesale replacement of a self-consistent tree without an independent
original anchor. Neither a receipt nor reconstructed relationships establish source authenticity, correctness of
undeclared ancestry, biomedical truth or scientific generalization. Exact historical launcher bytes matter even
when two revisions display the same version.
