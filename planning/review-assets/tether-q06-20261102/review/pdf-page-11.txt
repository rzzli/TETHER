6. Limitations
The twelve units were identity-ranked from a local archived subset, not randomly sampled from all biomedical
evidence. Their prior exposure is unresolved, source-study independence is unqualified and the selected rows
contain sufficient literal measurements. There is no certified holdout set, no generalization interval and no
clinical outcome evaluation. Public-download timestamps do not establish historical availability. The missing
Open Targets study input prevents upstream study-context verification.
The fourteen controls are known, designed and previously qualified. Passing them establishes only the
exercised behavior; unmodeled corruptions, adversarial combinations and real-world error prevalence remain
unmeasured. WIRE_ONLY and WITHOUT_WRAPPER are diagnostic projections, not complete alternative
agents. Multiple decisions from one output and multiple components from one unit are dependent. The copying
baseline is deliberately simple and its perfect performance is expected.
The later CLI counterexamples further limit any blanket semantic-verification claim. The frozen paper's
separate original-row and schedule audit still supports its stated mechanical result; it does not imply that the
legacy engine verified all coverage states or dossier assertions. Passing post-study regression tests qualifies
only the named software paths and exercised conditions. It does not supply unseen-fault generalization or a
new scientific sample.
Role separation, code pins and independent projection checks reduce identifiable implementation errors but do
not provide an operating-system sandbox, cryptographic provider identity or independent scientific
adjudication. AI agents assisted implementation, auditing, literature checking and writing. They are not external
experts, and their agreement does not certify biomedical correctness. Historical bespoke responses and
answer-aware descendants are excluded from this study rather than reused as references or labels.
The local model results are small negative development probes and cannot support model-family comparisons
or a general workflow ranking. The source study uses no model. Full Virtual Biotech reproduction, clinical
scoring, historical cohorts, MR, tasks T2-T5 and the H6 reanalysis remain separate uncompleted programmes.
Wider project data gaps are not resolved by finishing this technical experiment.
The P06 study and its results and costs remain frozen: primary production took 203.828 seconds and its
source audit took 7.125 seconds. A separately versioned, post-result P09 adapter has qualified installed,
relocated reconstruction on Windows x64 with CPython 3.11.15 and PyArrow 21.0.0. The native replay used all
137 exact originals; the complete adapter reproduced 38 outputs and 90 decisions across twelve native units
and fourteen constructed controls. Reconstruction took 289.938 seconds, and the separate source-inclusive
independent audit passed 6,862 checks in 15.187 seconds. The minimal public catalog creates new reference
fingerprints; historical equivalence removes only method.catalog_sha256. New G0 checks public protocol, code,
input and output byte bindings under independently retained authority, without establishing publisher
authenticity or verifying old private journals or sealed trees. Fresh exact-byte reacquisition of the 136 external
Open Targets originals remains untested. This local technical qualification establishes neither scientific validity
nor public availability; author facts remain deferred.
