S4. Fields, wire contract and endpoint
Each output declares schema_version, task_id, task_sha256, status, facts, collection_status and reason. A
fact declares fact_id, value, unit, source and unknown_reason. The source object binds source_id, file sha256,
release_id, physical locator and field. No prompt-only prose is scored as an emitted typed fact. Arrays preserve
order, integers/floats/Booleans remain distinct, duplicate JSON keys are rejected and oversized outputs fail
without truncation or repair.
Missingness reasons distinguish field_not_in_schema, null_source_value and empty_source_value. Native
empty values can be reportable or unknown only according to the declared field policy. Unit is null for these
native reporting tasks. An abstained or insufficient_input response has no facts and a reason. Wrong collection
status or a wrong value, unit, binding or missingness reason is a critical mechanical error. Unknown fact IDs
and duplicate fact emissions are errors.
C02's absent-standard-error example tests the generic JSON/core path only. The unchanged P05 native
Parquet scanner requires its declared measurement columns, so that control does not qualify native
absent-column acceptance or weaken the native schema policy. All eight unavailable native measurement
slots in this study are present-column nulls.
For an answerable group g, let n_g be its number of populated reference components and m_g the number of
exactly matched emitted components. Group coverage is m_g/n_g. Family coverage is the equal-weight mean
over groups with n_g greater than zero. Measurement completion requires an eligible answered/partial
response, correct collection status, no critical mechanical errors and mean measurement coverage at least 4/5.
Sensitivities use 3/5 and 1. A zero-answerable-group family has undefined coverage and cannot pass by 0/0.
The literal twelve-unit study has one answerable measurement group per unit. Technically valid C04 has none
and remains uncompleted despite exact reporting.
The native 80% and 100% criteria coincide for all attainable outcomes in all twelve units because ceil(0.8n_g) =
n_g for each observed group size. This is exact mathematical redundancy, not merely a consequence of the
observed perfect literal reports. The original prespecified thresholds and results remain unchanged; their
agreement supplies no independent robustness evidence.

The four native measurement fields are beta, standardError, pValueMantissa and pValueExponent. P-value
mantissa/exponent are reported separately; no p-value is recomputed. Exactness uses typed source-value
equality, not a scientific tolerance or inferred sign. Field-family coverage and exact all-fact reporting are
distinct. The missing-schema/empty behavior occurs in constructed controls and regressions; it is not falsely
reported as observed native missingness.
Strict exactness additionally requires a returned, structurally scorable report with status answered, zero critical
errors and exactly one emission of each declared slot. Status partial is permitted by the coverage-completion
contract but cannot satisfy strict exactness even with full coverage. Reference-value/null correctness, source
bindings, units, unknown reasons and collection status must all agree.
