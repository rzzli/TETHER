4.3 Verification and resource use
The separate source-inclusive auditor passes 2,414 checks with no unresolved finding. It independently
reconstructs original-row projections, constructed controls, policy decisions, source proofs and all-output
accounting. The frozen analyzer runs once against the pinned ledger and audit. A separate presentation audit
passes 351 checks over quantities, tables, captions, data and figure geometry. A fresh-directory local rebuild
reproduces all 39 result-bundle files byte-for-byte. Check counts describe implementation verification effort;
they are not biomedical sample sizes or an estimate of general correctness.
Production takes 203.828 seconds wall time and 198.984 seconds CPU, with checkpoint-observed
process-lifetime peak memory 305,827,840 bytes. The 197.812-second source-verification wrapper is included
within production, not an additional phase to sum. The separate audit takes 7.125 seconds and peaks at
333,893,632 bytes. Completed logical hash reads total 140,452,303,784 bytes in production and
2,897,268,566 in the audit; the two phases enumerate source rows twice in total. Physical I/O, partial
failed-read bytes and platform costs are unmeasured. Provider spending is $0, which is not a claim of zero
overall cost.
Source verification accounts for 97.0485% of production wall time (197.812/203.828). Production's completed
logical hash bytes equal 49.0021 source-frame equivalents (140,452,303,784/2,866,248,239). These are a
nested wall-time share and a logical-byte ratio, respectively. They measure neither physical disk transfers nor
agent-only inference cost. The repeated verification work dominates the reported production time; no
performance improvement is inferred from a later software release.
Reference construction is a separate preparatory phase: 342.750 seconds, with producer memory
unmeasured. Its later audit memory does not recover that missing measurement. Native literal/abstain
