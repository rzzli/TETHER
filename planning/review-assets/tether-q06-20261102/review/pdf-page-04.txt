The explicit schema-2 policy path in 0.5.0 checks exact artifact identity, use, release, temporal context and
supplied parents before offline extractive execution. It archives controls and receipt copies, produces a
restricted schema-1 compatibility projection, and rederives that projection against the actual engine inputs
during verification. A projection alone is not authorization. Direct legacy APIs and schema-1 compatibility runs
retain their earlier scope; model/replay/scorer use is unsupported by this policy path. Catalog collection and
scientific statuses remain unchanged. These are separate local software qualifications, not a rerun of the paper
experiment or evidence that its sources are scientifically sufficient.
3. Study design
3.1 Source frame and selection
We pinned Open Targets 25.09 evidence, credible-set, target and disease files [10] and the locally downloaded
HGNC normalization snapshot dated 10 October 2026 [11]. The HGNC date is a retained acquisition
designation, not a provider-certified annotation release. The frozen frame contains 137 original files totaling
2,866,248,239 bytes: 100 GWAS evidence files, 25 credible-set files, ten target files, one disease file and one
HGNC file. All source bytes and download receipts were verified. The complete credible-set input contains
2,862,043 physical rows.
The registry enumerates 1,753,850 physical GWAS evidence rows. Its fixed identity, temporal, mapping and
eligibility rules retain 1,681,738 rows and exclude 72,112; individual exclusion reasons can overlap. A
deterministic identity-only rank over target, disease and locus selects twelve units. Nine previously used target
symbols are excluded. Selection does not rank numerical values, source answerability or candidate
performance. Each retained unit has one exact locus-identifier match in the complete credible-set table. A
locus match is a technical join and does not establish shared study, cohort, allele orientation or biological
mechanism. Cross-source variant-identifier agreement is also unresolved: this is a locus-only match, not a
qualified composite study-and-variant match.
Broader prior project exposure is unknown for all twelve selected units. They are not certified fresh cases or
twelve independent studies. One repeated disease-identifier group is recorded; source-study and cohort
dependence remain unresolved. After execution their identities and results are exposed development material.
We preserve the pre-run exposure register rather than rewriting it with hindsight.
The rank is ascending SHA-256 of the UTF-8 string seed|release|targetId|diseaseId|studyLocusId, with seed
preprint-p05-deterministic-v1 and release 25.09. Lexical target, disease, locus and release fields break hash
ties. Missing, conflicting or blocked selected units retain their position and disposition; they are not replaced or
reranked to improve answerability or results.
3.2 Inputs and independent references
Each unit requests fifteen fields across its original GWAS and matched credible-set rows: eleven
identity/linkage fields and four measurement fields. The measurement components are beta, standard error,
p-value mantissa and p-value exponent, all in one declared measurement group per unit. We report these
native components without calculating a p-value, assigning effect units, interpreting direction or converting
provider scores into support weights. Sample size and other context can be retained without becoming graded
facts.
These are reporting components, not independent biological observations. The p-value mantissa and exponent
encode one p-value, and beta and standard error describe related properties of an estimate. Identity fields
account for most requested slots. Equal weighting is a declared reporting contract, not a measure of
independent evidence or clinical informativeness.
