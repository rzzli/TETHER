7. Availability and declarations
The repository contains implementation, frozen protocols, retained failures, auditable results, an editable
manuscript, bibliography and supplementary methods. At draft time, no public artifact deposit, permanent
identifier or publication is claimed. A public release must use an explicit allowlist; original datasets, model
weights, credentials, historical response archives and private or outcome-aware artifacts are not implicitly
licensed by the repository's Apache-2.0 code license. The supplement identifies local reconstruction
requirements and exact study anchors. Local file locations are implementation details, not public access
guarantees.
AI assistance: Codex-hosted agents supported software development, source checking, technical auditing and
manuscript preparation. The retained scientific report has no external-expert validation. Separate local-model
probes and their failures are disclosed above. Platform-model identity, hidden compute and platform cost are
not fully metered. No AI system is an author; human authors must verify and accept responsibility for the final
text and disclosures.
Author names, affiliations, correspondence, contributions, funding, competing interests, acknowledgments and
any required ethics/data-governance declarations require owner-confirmed facts. They are unresolved
submission metadata, not requests for external scientific experts. No statement of absent funding, conflicts or
oversight is inferred from missing information. No patients were recruited or clinical recommendations issued
by this technical experiment.
