4.1 Native availability and reporting
All twelve native units finalize. Of 180 requested facts, 148 are populated and 32 are native nulls: 108/132
identity slots and 40/48 measurement slots are populated. There are no missing-schema or native-empty
measurement slots in this set. One unit has two answerable measurement components, six have three, and
five have four. Beta is null in one unit and standard error in seven. Sample size is missing in ten units but is not
a graded measurement component.
The literal assembler emits all fifteen requested facts per unit with exact bindings and correct unknown
reasons. It achieves verified exact reporting and measurement completion in 12/12 units at each frozen
threshold. The abstention control completes 0/12, emitting no fact assertions and incurring no factual mismatch
errors. Abstention therefore provides error avoidance without useful completion. These contrasts establish the
expected copy ceiling and the explicit coverage cost of abstention on this task.
