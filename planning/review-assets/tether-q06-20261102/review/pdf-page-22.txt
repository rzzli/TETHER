S11. Subsequent source-composition feasibility and route limit
Q03 was a later source-only task/field audit, separate from the frozen experiment. Its repository evidence is
retained under planning/review-assets/tether-q03-20261102/. The initial task-spec/SOURCE-ONLY-TASK.md and
selection rule were frozen before biological row inspection. The rule chose the first eligible exact
disease/distinct ordered target pair lexicographically from the declared local evidence universe, without
selecting effects, scores, labels or a desired overlap pattern. Evidence coverage and credible-set join coverage
were tracked separately. This procedure establishes a deterministic selected example; it does not establish
independent sampling, a fresh holdout, or absence of broader prior source exposure.
The source-feasibility report (data/SOURCE-FEASIBILITY.md) and separately implemented review
(review/INDEPENDENT-FEASIBILITY-REVIEW.md) reconstruct four evidence rows, four matched credible-set rows
and three label rows. All four evidence-side study and variant IDs are null. Consequently, each of the four
study-ID comparisons and four variant-ID comparisons is unassessable, not equal or different. The credible-set
records supply three reported study IDs and three reported lead-variant IDs across four source objects; reuse
of these labels does not establish shared participants, independent replication, colocalisation or target
superiority. Source-object reporting is possible without filling the missing cross-table identities. Missing
effect/sample/cohort context applies to this selected slice, not every row of the release, and the absent study
table does not justify inventing upstream context.
