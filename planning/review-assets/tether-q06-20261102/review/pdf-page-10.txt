4.4 Negative model development, separate from the native study
Before narrowing scope, a frozen twelve-cell development comparison used two literature contexts with
deterministic, plain local-model and proposal/check arms plus a controlled missing-input condition. Five actual
local-model calls yielded four malformed responses and one output-limit failure; none satisfied the strict report
contract. Neither model arm completed either clean context, while the literal baseline completed 2/2. The
model was Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct in pinned Q4_K_M GGUF form, served through an ordinary completion
interface. Grammar-constrained generation was not used in this probe.
The five dispatches were three plain calls and two proposal calls; there were zero audit-stage dispatches.
Failure before that stage cannot measure critic efficacy. The configured output cap was 2,048 tokens, but
output-byte estimates did not establish that an exact valid serialization fit that token cap. A future generator
study would need the actual tokenizer, framing and escaping to establish feasibility under its unchanged
resource contract before execution. This requirement does not authorize retries of the closed cells.
A subsequent predeclared two-call fictional conformance probe passed its two-fact control and failed its
eight-fact control on the value/unknown-reason rule. The frozen stop rule closed this model branch. No further
prompt retries, model scaling or model evaluation on the twelve native units followed. These are exposed
development observations under one model/interface/configuration. They do not identify whether task design,
prompting, serialization, model capacity or their interaction caused failure, and they do not establish broad LLM
inability. Earlier zero-call setup failures are also retained.
5. Discussion
The study supports a limited engineering conclusion: selected native fields can be reported exactly while
eligibility, source ancestry, missingness, failure and resource records remain replayable. Explicit policies make
it possible to show which designed faults a weaker policy admits. Exact copying alone already reaches the
task's ceiling, so adding an agent cannot demonstrate superior biomedical interpretation on this endpoint. The
useful object is the inspectable reporting and verification contract, including its limits.
Prior work supplies substantial adjacent capabilities. The pinned Virtual Biotech implementation supports
headless execution and explicitly distinguishes artifact integrity from evidence coverage [12]. Alvessa already
verifies retrieved-record statements [4]. TxBench-PP addresses structured preclinical decisions [8], and
BiomniBench reports process rubrics [9]; we do not inherit their task validity or judge calibration. Retrieve to
Explain evaluates evidence-driven target prediction against different endpoints [13]. None was executed here.
Missing feature assessments are not evidence of competitor deficiencies. Our bounded contribution is
therefore an implementation and source-reporting feasibility result, with incremental utility and scientific value
still to establish.
The missing context is substantive. Exact locus matches and literal beta/standard-error components do not
establish compatible study identity, ancestry, genome build, allele orientation, units, intervention direction or
causal effect. A reference assembled from a public database can faithfully reproduce an upstream error. The
constructed finite negative standard-error example makes this distinction explicit. A clinical predictor,
Mendelian-randomization analysis or therapeutic ranking would require additional data qualification and
scientific validation before an outcome study.
