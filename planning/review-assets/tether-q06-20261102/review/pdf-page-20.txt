316 displayed rows, three vector PDF/SVG figures, PNG previews, exact figure data and captions, plus
ARTIFACTS.json. The CSVs retain full serialized numeric precision; Markdown rounds display values.
Unknown cells stay unknown rather than zero. The manifest binds every generated payload to its exact
analyzed source. See the original results guide for the local rebuild command and resource semantics. This
revision preserves all original result payloads and archived releases. Its PDF/package require separate dated
qualification records; manuscript changes alone do not establish those checks.

S9. Availability, rights and unresolved author metadata
No public deposit, release DOI, hosted exact-release result or publication is claimed in this draft. Apache-2.0
applies to project code; external provider and underlying-source terms require product-specific review. The
qualified local allowlist includes the exact CC0 HGNC original and project receipt metadata; 136 Open Targets
originals remain external. This narrow inclusion is not general dataset redistribution clearance. Credentials,
private machine paths, historical bespoke responses and their descendants, raw clinical outcomes and
unreviewed model/data bytes are excluded. A local artifact can contain evidence not licensed for redistribution;
its presence is not clearance.
Author names/affiliations, corresponding author, actual contributions, funding, competing interests and
acknowledgments need factual owner confirmation before submission. AI assistance is disclosed in the main
text; final human authors remain responsible for content. The draft does not infer a no-conflict or no-funding
declaration. No new external scientific expert input is required. Scientific validation, study independence and
clinical utility remain unestablished even after technical paper packaging is finished.
