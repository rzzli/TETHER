S3. Selection, exposure and dependence
The registry scans all 1,753,850 local GWAS physical rows. Fixed rules retain 1,681,738 eligible rows and
exclude 72,112. Eligibility reasons can overlap, so a summed reason histogram is not the number of excluded
rows. Target/disease/locus identity rank is fixed without measurement-value or candidate-performance
ranking. The nine excluded previously used symbols are CD276, OSMR, ERBB2, PCSK9, IL6R, CFTR,
GLP1R, SCN9A and CTLA4.
For each eligible unique release/target/disease/locus unit, the rank key is SHA-256 of the UTF-8 concatenation
preprint-p05-deterministic-v1|25.09|targetId|diseaseId|studyLocusId. Ascending hash order is followed by
lexical targetId, diseaseId, studyLocusId and release_id tie order; the first twelve units are selected. Missing,
conflicting, blocked or insufficient selected units keep their position, failure or missingness. There is no
replacement, answerability reranking or numeric-performance selection after the freeze.
The units are p05-01 through p05-12, with one original GWAS and one exact-locus credible-set row per unit.
The evaluator register contains 180 requested slots: 132 identity and 48 measurement. Native nulls are 24
identity and eight measurement. Per-unit answerable measurement group sizes are 3,3,4,4,4,3,3,2,4,3,3,4.
Identity and measurement components are not independent observations. Twelve locus strings and one
repeated disease string group do not establish twelve independent studies or cohorts.
The p-value mantissa and exponent represent one quantity in two components; beta and standard error are
also related. The 180 slots are a reporting denominator, not 180 independent observations. Equal-weight
group/family summaries do not establish clinical informativeness, and source-study/cohort independence is
unresolved. Deterministic prospective selection fixes the local selection procedure; it does not create a random
sample, erase earlier exposure or certify a holdout population.
Broader exposure is UNKNOWN in the frozen pre-run register. After the study all twelve outputs and
underlying identities are exposed. New holdout claims cannot be created by moving them to a different folder.
Old bespoke review responses, normalized responses, candidate assessments, answer-aware mappings and
descendants are archive-only and excluded from generation, reference construction, judging, tuning and
source/case/arm selection. This exclusion is a declared input policy with retained ancestry checks, not
evidence that an AI model never encountered a public source during training.
