## Important feasibility limit

Both policies selected the **same one record**,
`ot25:europepmc:33fa583c68da688b017cb8c99a1bce21811d9034`, and excluded four under
the whole-request byte bound. There is no observed policy contrast in this frame.
Two records exceed 12,000 bytes even alone. Three fit individually, but every
pair containing the selected record exceeds the bound (15,431–21,605 bytes).
[Bound diagnostic](BOUND-DIAGNOSTIC.json) distinguishes these individual and
cumulative limits. Exclusion is not always individual oversize, irrelevance or
negative evidence. No source was clipped and no limit increased to force success.

The check establishes a real multi-candidate collection and its admission/selection
mechanics. It does not show scientifically sufficient coverage or retrieval
quality. Five integrated records are not proof of five independent studies.
No answer, native output, four-arm real comparison, MR feature or clinical
probability was generated. Real four-arm manifests remain preparation metadata.
