## Limits that remain even when tests pass

The Python open hook audits managed opens in the trusted owned worker. It is not OS isolation, a malicious-code sandbox, or proof against same-user/native-library access. Trusted preparation/checking may reconstruct labeled archive members; that is separate from predictor access. Worker recipes omit private manifests and evaluator products.

Memory is observed by a 0.5-second working-set poll with OS lifetime peak where available. The mechanism can overshoot between samples and is not a kernel memory cap. Parent deadlines bound the owned child, with separately disclosed cleanup; they do not attest arbitrary descendants or host behavior. Unknown/unsampled metrics stay unknown. Declared product bytes and primary-output bytes exclude other reads, journal/log overhead and physical I/O; the full dependency scans consume measured stage time.

Source proofs bind externally anchored original receipts, exact projection bytes, native purpose grants, model/input artifacts, producer events and checked candidates. They do not mathematically prove coefficient learning or semantic entailment. The new native task contract reuses real TETHER primitives but does not inherit all therapeutic schema-2/BG02 guarantees. Public-development annotation agreement is neither private holdout validity nor biological truth; no natural result exists at this checkpoint.
