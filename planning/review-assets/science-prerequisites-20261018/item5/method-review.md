## What the current example can mean

The example is BLUEPRINT QTS000002/QTD000021 **gene-level IL6R transcript abundance in naive monocytes**, paired with GCST90132223 **European rheumatoid arthritis susceptibility**. It does not measure an identified protein isoform, soluble or membrane receptor concentration, receptor activity, antibody blockade, treatment response or clinical-trial success. Gene-level transcript abundance must not be described as a measured individual transcript isoform. The reported exposure N=191 and GWAS 22,350 cases/74,823 controls are study descriptors, not automatically per-variant/effective sample sizes. QTL `an` counts alleles and must not become individual N without validation.

Compared with the existing full-cis feasibility assessment, the important advance here is separation of three questions: whether regional bytes and LD arithmetic reproduce, whether the selected statistical method has its actual required inputs, and whether causal/therapeutic interpretation and historical availability qualify. Passing the first question cannot answer the latter two.

Association, colocalization and MR remain separate evidence fields. A phenotype association does not assign a causal gene. Regional colocalization compares support for shared versus distinct causal signals under a model. MR evaluates an exposure-outcome hypothesis under instrumental-variable assumptions. Shared signals can arise without exposure mediation. Transcript MR sign cannot direct inhibition/activation or become a clinical probability. [Original colocalization method](https://journals.plos.org/plosgenetics/article?id=10.1371/journal.pgen.1004383), [MR guidance, limited-access details below](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/7384151/).

## Method-specific input decisions

| Route | Statistical inputs actually used | Separate scientific qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Association display | Defined phenotype/study and variant identity; reported estimate/SE or valid association result | Source trust, scale, multiplicity, study/population QC; no gene or drug assignment from P alone |
| `coloc.abf`, beta/variance | Dense shared variant identities; beta, SE squared and trait type; quantitative sdY known or estimated by a justified route | Coverage, single-causal assumption, priors and study compatibility; no universal LD requirement |
| `coloc.abf`, P approximation | P, MAF, N, trait type; binary case fraction | Approximation/scale/imputation limitations; no invented GWAS N/EAF |
| `coloc::runsusie` then `coloc.susie` | Compatible signed LD in exact SNP/effect-allele order, qualified study N, signed association information and wrapper-specific fields | LD sampling/mismatch, multiple-signal model, convergence, credible sets and every signal-pair comparison |
| Single-instrument cis MR | Qualified chosen instrument, harmonized exposure/outcome beta/SE, specified uncertainty and overlap/covariance assumptions | Relevance, independence and exclusion restriction; weak denominator, winner's curse, intervention correspondence |
| Multi-instrument cis MR | Defined estimator, beta/SE, independent-instrument justification or compatible signed LD/covariance | Correlated/joint strength, numerical conditioning, heterogeneity and appropriate pleiotropy/weak-instrument sensitivity |

For beta/variance case-control ABF, the executable official source requires neither N/MAF nor case fraction for that arithmetic. The generic help page's compressed description of `s` is broader than the source's conditional check; the minimal vignette agrees with the source. Case/control counts and sample composition still matter to study QC. Quantitative sdY=1 requires evidence that the exact analyzed scale supports it. Current Catalogue documentation describes inverse-normal transformation, but cannot alone qualify the exact retained release or residual scale. [Input-check source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chr1swallace/coloc/main/R/check.R), [ABF source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chr1swallace/coloc/main/R/claudia.R), [eQTL methods](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/eqtl/Methods/).

`coloc::runsusie` explicitly needs N; the lower-level current `susie_rss` interface describes some n-absent paths. These are different contracts. Neither permits substituting 503 LD-reference individuals for association-study N. The retained 2,979-by-2,979 correlation matrix may properly be singular with 503 centered genotype samples. A matrix can be reproducible while being unsuitable for a selected inverse-based estimator or for either association study. Prespecify any rank handling and residual-variance options; diagnose signed allele alignment, LD mismatch and reference-panel uncertainty. [Wrapper requirements](https://chr1swallace.github.io/coloc/reference/runsusie.html), [lower-level RSS documentation](https://stephenslab.github.io/susieR/reference/susie_rss.html).

The MR contract explicitly states relevance, independence from confounding pathways, exclusion restriction and intervention/transport assumptions. Per-instrument approximate F can be computed from exposure beta/SE, but is not joint strength for many correlated instruments. No p-value threshold may be relaxed after inspecting outcomes. A single instrument cannot identify horizontal pleiotropy by an Egger intercept or heterogeneity test. Unknown participant overlap is not zero; overlap and weakness can alter bias and uncertainty. No one sensitivity method proves causal assumptions. [Primary overlap study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27625185/), [MR author guidance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/7384151/).
