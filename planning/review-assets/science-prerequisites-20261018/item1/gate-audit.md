# Item 1 scientific gate audit — 2026-10-18

Scope: read-only review of the seven-stage qualification proposal against existing local protocols, code and retained evidence. This audit proposes gates; it does not activate clinical evaluation, acquire inputs, restore a database, derive genetics features, train a scorer or qualify new biological claims. Provider spending is $0. AI-agent scientific interpretation is source-grounded review, not external validation or proof of causal mechanism.

## Findings that change the proposed plan

1. **Chronology must be checked per feature origin, not only the final test origin.** `tools/feasibility/chronology_policy.py::validate_protocol` currently checks all declared model releases against January 2025, but has no training/calibration-row origins. That is adequate for validating its explicitly unactivated local candidate protocol; it does not certify a historical training feature table. Qwen's September 2024 immutable release can qualify in principle for January 2025 prediction, but cannot create a 2023-as-of LLM feature. Recomputing deterministic features today from complete eligible 2023 input lineage is permitted. A contemporary coding/review agent must not supply outcome-informed biological facts to those derivations. The official release announcement is September 19, 2024; the existing exact-GGUF pin remains the operative asset proof. [Qwen announcement](https://qwenlm.github.io/blog/qwen2.5/).

2. **Endpoint and reporting cutoff are different dates.** Keep the candidate event window January 1, 2025 to January 1, 2026. Proposed ascertainment through April 1, 2026 is a provisional 90-day reporting allowance, not an extended event window and not proof reporting is complete. A March 2026 trial start falls outside the endpoint even if observed by April. A December 2025 actual start first posted in February may enter only under a frozen lag rule and qualified matching. Dump timestamps/timezones and archive-publication precision remain explicit. January 2026 alone cannot establish three months of subsequent reporting completeness.

3. **A recorded later-phase start is not clinical success.** Freeze separate endpoint IDs for registry-recorded next-phase actual start, documented efficacy/success and cessation/failure, with source-specific event dates and availability dates. No-recorded-start is an unknown unless a documented ascertainment/censoring rule supports a negative for that specific endpoint. Cessation for commercial reasons is not biological failure. Never derive clinical-success labels from the registry advancement surrogate.

4. **The existing reference gap is qualification, not absence of any review.** Ten cases/20 claims already have AI-assisted assessments, but are development-exposed and unadjudicated. The user now authorizes agent panels; the next stage can produce independent-task, blinded, source-cited AI adjudications without demanding unavailable human expertise. Record model families, shared training, packet exposure and disagreements. Such process separation does not make model errors statistically independent or establish externally adjudicated truth. Mechanical source-support questions can be resolved against original rows; action/modality relevance and causal interpretation can legitimately remain uncertain. Do not force a human-only gate and do not call consensus externally independent validation.

5. **Current power is a warning, not a new-window estimate.** Existing synthetic normal-score simulations on older proxy labels support a revise decision. They do not estimate actual 2025–2026 power, validated prevalence or model performance. Recompute only after new-window outcome ascertainment and dependence definitions qualify. Preserve 80% power for a 0.05 AUROC difference as the current planning target; freeze multiplicity, effect assumptions and calibration-precision criteria before model comparisons. Correlation and assumed label error must not be tuned to obtain a pass. Assess precision of calibration intercept/slope and Brier separately; AUROC power alone does not justify calibration claims.

6. **Genetics limitations are method-specific.** The retained 2,979-variant/503-EUR-sample bundle passed technical checks but has unresolved publisher-MD5 mismatch, unqualified overlap/study-specific LD, missing GWAS per-variant N/EAF and unqualified historical eligibility. These are not interchangeable blockers. `coloc.abf` can use beta/varbeta without LD; requirements differ by quantitative/case-control trait and known versus estimated sdY. N/MAF become needed for certain approximation routes; absence of per-SNP EAF does not universally prohibit all methods. SuSiE-based colocalization does require aligned signed LD. A naive-monocyte transcript exposure must not be relabeled protein abundance or antibody inhibition. Source-trust qualification and causal applicability remain separate from computational reproducibility. [coloc input requirements](https://chr1swallace.github.io/coloc/reference/check_dataset.html), [coloc data vignette](https://chr1swallace.github.io/coloc/articles/a02_data.html), [SuSiE colocalization](https://chr1swallace.github.io/coloc/articles/a06_SuSiE.html).

7. **Archived false flags are not current runtime failure.** `evaluation-v0.2.json` preserves planned collection/runtime fields and all clinical activation gates false. Later runtime source-verification artifacts establish engineering operation, but do not retroactively activate the archived protocol. Add a new amendment/evidence mapping rather than rewriting its freeze. Likewise Task 5's completed checkbox means its conditional no-go assessment completed, not scientific derivation/training completed.

## Exact chronology and eligibility rules to implement downstream

- For each feature row, `information_available_at <= row_origin`; every annotation, mapping, assay definition and computational source ancestor must qualify. An imprecise date interval qualifies only if its latest possible availability is no later than origin. `computed_at` can be later than origin when no later information enters.
- For every contributing LLM role, pin immutable weights, tokenizer/config, prompt, tools, runtime and adaptations. Weight release bound must precede that row's origin; unknown exact training cutoff is disclosed rather than invented. Later runtime binaries alone need not carry later scientific knowledge, but later fine-tunes, embeddings, retrieval knowledge, model-written feature annotations and role outputs are information-bearing inputs and require eligibility checks.
- Training/calibration event dates and **label ascertainment availability dates** must precede candidate deployment/test origin. Do not use a full later archive to fabricate an earlier availability date. Completed two-year follow-up from a 2023 origin may not be available by January 1, 2025 once reporting lag is added; shorten or shift development windows explicitly, or retain no training route.
- Freeze split assignment by program/drug/dependence components before fitting transformations. Fit imputation, normalization, feature selection and calibration on their permitted data only. Report target/sponsor dependence stress tests without assuming one giant component is necessarily the sole valid scientific unit.
- For T6 contemporary references, separate source facts from judgment and applicability. Current literature may inform a contemporary reference, but cannot flow back into historical predictors. An independently assigned AI task is not external human adjudication; report what the actual design supports.

## Method reporting and uncertainty

Use STROBE-MR to disclose genetics assumptions and TRIPOD+AI to structure prediction reporting. Neither checklist certifies validity; program-level prediction is an adaptation of clinical prediction reporting, not identical patient-level use. [STROBE-MR](https://www.bmj.com/content/375/bmj.n2233), [TRIPOD+AI](https://www.bmj.com/content/385/bmj-2023-078378).

The inspected code's existing chronology tests reject future contributors, missing roles, mutable revisions, later fine-tunes, evaluator-only/current fallback predictors and unearned lock/public registration. Add row-origin, lag-boundary, training-label-availability, source-gap and correlated-review examples in downstream stages. This audit does not change or run the production pipeline and does not assert a statistical or scientific gate has passed.
