## Accepted scope

The next scientific milestone is a bounded **contemporary T6 interpretation
feasibility pilot**, followed by an independently sized H5 evaluation if its gates
pass. The immediate research question is whether the system preserves supplied
mechanism context, distinguishes observations from proposals, and gives useful
uncertainty within a declared evidence and compute budget.

The [recovery plan](../Project%20Recovery%20and%20Evaluation%20Plan%202026-10-26.md)
defines the ordered work. Original hypotheses and tasks in the
[original proposal](../Beyond%20the%20Virtual%20Biotech%20Tooling%20Gaps%20and%20Project%20Plan.md)
remain unchanged and reachable. A narrower current phase does not complete the
original programme.

## Resource and governance decisions

1. **Model control:** prepare matched existing/simplified-interface and capability
   conditions using already authorized assets. Provider spending remains $0.
   Inspect hardware and assets before proposing a larger local model. No new paid
   API, model download, license or paid expert commitment is authorized here.
2. **Human reference:** the owner arranges relevant reviewers; agents prepare
   packets, assignment, masking and analysis. Two relevant independent judgments
   are needed per case, with a third adjudicator for substantive disagreements.
   AI reviews stay labeled AI development assessments. Missing humans do not
   block Steps 1–6 engineering, but do block independent scientific acceptance.
3. **Clinical route:** original H1–H4 are deferred until a named source passes
   outcome, mapping, chronology, dependence and power qualification. Existing
   AACT archives should not be requested again without a specific verified gap.
4. **Development governance:** bounded plan, appropriate regressions, proportional
   review, exact failure/accounting record and concise closeout. Reuse qualified
   infrastructure instead of repeating controller stacks.
5. **Confirmatory governance:** freeze sources/cases/questions/model/analysis;
   use independent source-only references and masked review; retain disagreements,
   failures, once-only execution, semantic verification and tested restoration.
6. **Code preservation:** complete the checkpoint and archive compatibility work
   before modifying production modules used by pinned historical verifiers. Keep
   original receipts, source bytes, failed attempts and raw transport receipts.
7. **Operational limits:** existing budgets and $0 provider spending persist.
   No automatic generation retry, response repair, new bulk acquisition, download
   worker change or periodic-monitor restart.

## Acceptance and stop rules

Step 1 is complete when this decision, the initial exposure register and the live
TODO reference every original hypothesis/task and preserve their reopening gates.
This is an engineering scope decision, not a scientific finding.

Stop a dependent experiment if source identity, lineage, archive restoration,
budget or required reference fails. Missing human reviews block the scientific
acceptance gate; agents must not manufacture them. Missing file/access/permission
needs are presented as exact, reviewable requests after independent preparation.
Null probabilities and empty unvalidated prediction features remain unchanged.
