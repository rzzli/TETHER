# Decision to remove external expert inputs

31 October 2026, America/Los_Angeles. Recorded under the owner's explicit instruction to redo planning with **no more external expert input** and to remove human responses as an input. This supersedes the human recruitment, response, follow-up, confirmation and panel requirements in the October 26 recovery plan and subsequent handoffs. No further reviewer submission, GE source-first confirmation, expert adjudication or reviewer scheduling is a project dependency.

## Active scope

The next deliverable is a source-grounded, automatically checked contemporary T6 evaluation, supported by disclosed AI reference construction and adversarial AI review. The [new execution plan](../AI%20Only%20Evaluation%20Plan%202026-10-31.md) governs current work. Agents can conduct source research and evaluation without arranging outside experts. Only genuine file/access/permission or measured resource failures require owner intervention; scientific disagreement is preserved as uncertainty or an excluded claim.

The amended **H5-A** evaluates source-traceable completion, coverage of mechanically answerable facts and error handling. AI reference concordance and AI preference are secondary, explicitly model-conditioned results. The original H5 claim of blinded expert preference and lower expert-audited error/cost remains historically defined and **untested**, outside this phase. Original H1–H4/H6 are neither deleted nor completed by this change.

## Input policy

- Existing bespoke expert responses, addenda, normalizations, ratings, comparisons, response-aware source annotations and all their descendants are **archive-only**. They do not enter future references, model inputs, evaluator inputs, tuning, rubric design or case/arm selection. Preserve originals and earlier provenance classifications unchanged.
- This conversation and planning context have seen response summaries. Source-reference annotators must start in fresh contexts with an explicit source-only allowlist; they must not inherit this chat, project memory, returned responses, prior answer-aware annotations or candidate answers.
- Pre-response raw articles, original source cards and source-only question definitions remain eligible for **exposed development** if their provenance establishes that they predate the responses. A source chosen or ranked because of a returned answer is not clean input. No existing case becomes fresh through this amendment.
- Published primary sources and pre-existing curated benchmark releases are eligible research datasets, subject to rights, versions, source lineage and exposure checks. They are not newly commissioned expert responses. Public labels are optional and task-specific; no published benchmark is assumed present or clinically valid without inspection.
- New AI answers and reconciliation records remain `evaluation_only`, separate from generation/retrieval sources. Separate agents do not by themselves establish independent errors, external scientific validation or human equivalence.

## Boundaries retained

No accepted source/runtime contract is weakened. Historical `human_scientific_review=pending` fields and their verifiers remain unchanged; a new prospective policy records that the human endpoint is retired, rather than falsely satisfied. R07 remains six complete responses with zero frozen-contract passes. Native recognition remains FAILED and whole verification INCOMPLETE_TIMEOUT. No response repair or automatic replay is authorized.

Provider spending remains $0. Existing platform agents may perform disclosed research/reference work; unknown platform usage/cost is not called free or a matched model budget. This decision authorizes no paid API, new model/bulk-data download, live model adapter, protected clinical-outcome read, native-proof expansion, downloader change or monitoring restart. Current real numerical prediction features stay empty and probabilities null.

## Disposition of the prior human workstream

HR1–HR4 and the outstanding candidate-follow-up request are **retired from active scope**, not marked successfully completed. Existing intake work remains valid archival provenance. Missing new human responses no longer block the project. Source eligibility, objective measurability, AI provenance, temporal/lineage integrity, model access, budget and statistical identifiability remain actual gates; if they fail, report a bounded negative result without introducing another expert dependency.
