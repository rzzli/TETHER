# AI only evaluation plan

31 October 2026, America/Los_Angeles. **Current controlling plan.** The owner has removed human responses and external expert participation from project inputs and dependencies. This plan supersedes those parts of the [October 26 recovery plan](Project%20Recovery%20and%20Evaluation%20Plan%202026-10-26.md), HR1–HR4 and all later requests for reviewer confirmations or follow-up. The [scope decision](decisions/2026-10-31-no-external-expert-input.md) records the change. No external expert recruitment, response collection, panel or adjudication is required at any future step.

The project continues toward auditable target-evaluation dossiers, scientifically bounded evaluation and the conditional clinical/genetics programme. Removing reviewers does not create missing biological data, qualify a model runtime or establish clinical truth. The immediate study measures **reporting fidelity to declared sources**. It can finish with a useful positive, negative or inconclusive result without expert input.

## 2. Amended question and endpoints

**H5-A:** Under fixed evidence, questions and budgets, does the interpretation workflow improve source-traceable completion and coverage of prospectively enumerated, mechanically checkable fact slots relative to the declared simple baselines and an honestly adapted Virtual Biotech arm?

The original H5 expert-preference hypothesis remains untested and is not reported as completed. Clinical efficacy, causal validity, usefulness to practicing scientists, expert equivalence and clinical superiority are outside H5-A. A generator can accurately report a flawed paper; this endpoint does not certify that paper's biology.

| Endpoint | Definition and denominator | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Primary: mechanically verified completion with typed-fact coverage | Across the frozen answerable cases with at least one objective slot, a terminal answer passes the common scoring contract, required mechanical integrity checks, zero mechanically established critical distortions, and at least 80% of objectively answerable fact slots. The same denominator applies to every arm; failures/timeouts count as non-completions | An engineering utility floor, not a validated clinical threshold; passing does not certify every free-text proposition |
| Fact-slot coverage | Correctly reported required slots divided by all frozen objectively answerable slots, per case; permitted equivalent representations specified in advance | Limited to facts that the source and transformation rules actually establish |
| Critical structured errors | Wrong target/disease/scope identifier, altered value/sign/interval, invented explicit unit, broken source locator or ineligible lineage where mechanically decidable | Source-reporting error, not a test of causal truth |
| Missing-input behavior | Report abstention/partial answers and unsupported assertions separately in a prospectively defined insufficient-evidence stratum | A system cannot win solely by abstaining on all answerable cases |
| Citation mechanics | Exact-span replay, valid source IDs and proportion of structured claims with resolvable citations; all structured claims form the denominator | Citation presence/quote equality does not establish entailment |
| AI semantic audit | Source support, attribution, omissions, mechanism transfer and uncertainty, with quoted rationale; report each judge and unresolved disagreements | A model-conditioned proxy, not an objective error label |
| AI paired preference | Neutral labels, predetermined order swaps, ties/neither/uncertain allowed | Secondary only; never renamed human preference |
| Resource use | Completion rate, latency, peak memory, tokens/bytes and actual versus conservative accounting; source/reference/evaluation overhead separate | Unknown platform charges/tokens remain unknown; no total-cost superiority claim from $0 API spending |

The 80% floor is a **prospective development choice**, not an estimate of acceptable biomedical error. Report its full coverage distribution and a predeclared sensitivity analysis at 60% and 100%; do not choose a threshold after viewing arm performance. Each case's slot set and answerability stratum are fixed before candidate generation. Admit primary slots only from typed raw source fields or finite source syntax with transparently reproducible transformation rules and separately checked implementations. AI-only semantic assignment of a field's meaning does not create objective truth and stays secondary. Broad semantic interpretation stays outside the primary score. Zero-slot cases have no defined coverage fraction: `0/0` never passes. Use separately frozen observable missingness/state rules for those cases, and report them and all-cell terminal accounting separately from the answerable-case primary denominator.

Freeze a common scoring representation and each arm's deterministic adapter before seeing new outputs. Baselines need not emit the interpretation backend's native wire format. Adapters may expose facts and citations already emitted, but must not add missing facts, repair claims, substitute source text for an absent answer or hide invalid/failed outputs. Retain originals and map every scored field back to its original location. Any contract-validity measure specific to one generator is reported separately from the cross-arm primary endpoint.

Use case-level paired results and target-family/source-study clusters. Claims, passages, related hypotheses and multiple judges are not independent sample units. Failures, invalid outputs, abstentions, ties, exclusions and missing ratings remain visible with distinct denominators. Intervals quantify variation over the sampled cases under this measurement procedure; they cannot eliminate systematic source or judge error.

## 3. Inputs and exclusion policy

**Allowed:** receipt-verified raw source records, their pre-response mappings/annotations, source-only excerpts, fixed hypothesis/question definitions, predeclared synthetic controls, and optionally task-compatible public benchmark releases after access/rights/version/label/exposure checks. Public labels may historically have been human-curated; they are fixed dataset artifacts rather than bespoke expert responses. No public benchmark is required for the initial exposed-source development route.

**Archive-only:** all received expert responses, addenda, normalized returns, provenance-derived comparisons, candidate ratings, response-aware AI annotations and their descendants. Exclude them from new references, generation, judging, prompt/rubric tuning, source ranking and case/arm selection. Preserve their original roles, hashes and history. Keep the earlier intake reports as archival audit evidence, not evaluation inputs.

The current planning context has seen response summaries. Future reference agents must use **fresh contexts with no conversation fork**, receiving only an allowlisted source bundle and neutral task/rubric. They do not read current memory, this plan's response history, other reference proposals, candidates or old AI triage. Procedural separation is not an OS security boundary or proof of independent model errors. An allowlist/source-role test must detect direct and derived response leakage before annotation or generation. When a clean lineage cannot be demonstrated, exclude the affected item and record why; do not ask an expert to cure it.

Pre-response source cards may be reused for exposed development after their exact source/request/question hashes are checked. Old candidates already exist, so later source-only AI construction cannot make their evaluation prospective. No old case, source article or related target/action/modality hypothesis is called fresh by renaming it. A source selected in response to an expert's answer is excluded even if the article itself is public.

New references, judge outputs, mask keys and controlled expected answers are `evaluation_only` through every derived parent. Generation receives sources and questions, never evaluation answers. The existing catalog's evaluator products can express this role; no new catalog collection/scientific-validation status is implied by this planning change.

## 4. AI review procedure

1. **Source compiler:** resolve source identity, version, cutoff, rights/access depth, original row/span locators and complete mappings. Enumerate mechanically checkable slots and explicit unknowns without candidate access. Full text and external primary sources can be investigated by research agents, but must enter as separately versioned dated sources, never silent repairs of frozen cards.
2. **Annotator A and annotator B:** use two fresh source-only contexts. Record execution ID, model identity/configuration when available, unknown-identity reason, supplied input hashes, role and access boundary. Each answers the same structured questions with exact source pointers and confidence/uncertainty, independently of the other's proposal and candidates.
3. **Mechanical checker:** reproduce each quoted span and structured value, validate allowed fields and scope, reject structurally invented context and enforce predeclared unknown fields and evaluator isolation. Mechanical pass certifies only those checks. Semantic entailment is not inferred from substring equality.
4. **Reconciler:** a separate fresh context sees the sealed source proposals only after both are retained. Give source-grounded reasons for each resolution; preserve alternatives and unresolved items. No majority vote turns a proposition into truth. A deterministic fact cannot be overturned by AI preference; a mechanical contradiction triggers repair of the new evaluator or an explicit invalid-reference state before generation.
5. **Candidate auditor:** after reference sealing, evaluate frozen outputs using only the declared sources/reference/rubric and anonymous candidate labels, without generator identity or human-response history. Source-reference construction and candidate auditing are separate stages. For old R07 outputs, label this retrospective and exposed.
6. **Bias checks:** preassign both A/B display orders; use fixed controls for verbosity, style, citation substitution, numerical corruption, unsupported action transfer and insufficient evidence. Preserve every scheduled judgment, not only favorable ones. Report order-inconsistency and individual judge differences.

Different prompts or agents using the same model family are correlated checks. Cross-family diversity is useful when already available but is **not a new external-file, account or purchase prerequisite**. If only one family is available, continue with mechanical endpoints and clearly exploratory semantic audits. If exact model/version or usage is unavailable in platform-agent work, disclose that and do not represent it as a reproducible capability ceiling or matched experimental generator arm.

A single-model study may proceed as explicitly model-conditioned if otherwise feasible; absent a stronger control, do not attribute failure uniquely to model capacity versus task/interface design.

Separate measured local cost, platform-agent usage when observable, unknown/unmetered work, reference construction, indexing and judge overhead. ## 8. Why this evaluation needs two kinds of evidence

This is a project design decision, not a conclusion that AI reviewers are equivalent to scientists. Prior evaluation research documents position, verbosity and self-preference concerns for model judges; hence predeclared order swaps, provenance and separate mechanical endpoints. See [Zheng et al., Judging LLM-as-a-Judge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685).

[ALCE](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/) separates correctness and citation quality in automated evaluation. It motivates checking source pointers alongside fact reporting; its general-domain measurements do not validate this project's biomedical semantics. [ARES](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.20/) uses human-labeled data for its prediction-powered inference correction. We do not inherit that correction or its claims by removing our human inputs. H5-A intervals remain conditional on this project's actual objective measurements and admitted data, with uncalibrated AI semantic scores reported separately.

No framework source, legacy verifier, model runtime, original dataset or expert response is changed by this planning deliverable. The retained application acceptance remains 0.3.11/default v2 with 1,774 tests; those tests are not newly rerun for prose changes. Downloader/stopped-monitor boundaries and original native FAILED/INCOMPLETE_TIMEOUT remain unchanged. The next concrete work is A02, which no longer waits for an external expert response.
