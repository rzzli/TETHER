## Reassessed design and data feasibility

Source availability and successful interpretation are separate. Both original
source records, their offered scientific passages and their provenance are already
available and receipt-bound. The actual native prompts included all offered
passages and explicit positive instructions to read experiments and context. The
failure cannot be attributed simply to missing snippets or a transport truncation.
The65 incomplete collection inputs remain explicit and must not imply biological
absence. Complete primary methodological context and fresh scientific truth remain
separate limitations.

Task names such as mechanism-context and discovery-replication were orchestration
labels; their distinct scientific questions were not separately present in the
model request beyond shared hypothesis/instructions. Extensive annotation and
provenance fields and shared-field inheritance may also affect salience. These are
candidate design explanations, not proven causes. Two deliberately exposed tasks
cannot establish general model incapacity, broader T6/H5 performance or superiority
of a replacement design.

The next stage can use the same public, pinned local source records. It needs no
new download, clinical outcome, paid API, model weight or evaluator-only enrichment.
Earlier primary-source qualifications stay evaluation-only; copying their answers
or excerpts into agent input would violate lineage/role separation. A new source
acquisition would need its own scope and provenance, outside this stage.

## Ordered next stage

1. **Specify source questions before changing runtime.** Define generic questions by
   evidence family, independent of case-specific answers. Bind the question,
   hypothesis, selected source IDs and source scope into the request fingerprint.
   A mechanism question can ask what manipulations/models/comparators/endpoints
   are actually reported. A genetics question can conditionally ask about reported
   discovery/replication, exposure direction, uncertainty and transfer limits.
   Neither may presume that missing fields or a failed replication prove absence.
2. **Review a lossless scientific-first view.** Present reported passages distinctly
   from provider annotations and operational provenance. Preserve every admitted
   field, null/absence/type distinction and original pointer/quote locator. Do not
   rank or select passages using evaluator judgments, drop inconvenient findings,
   silently replace source content or turn provider scores into effect features.
3. **Specify coherent assertions and abstention.** Keep the question being assessed
   distinct from an endorsed scientific proposition. An abstention must not silently
   assert the hypothesis in a structured tuple. Preserve useful positive inference
   and contextual caution as possible outcomes; an all-abstention workaround fails.
   Define migration/archival verification before changing the sidecar contract.
4. **Implement and validate only after scientific/methods specification review.**
   Add meaningful synthetic regressions for question binding, same-source/different-
   question separation, full source preservation, quote locations, contradictory
   structured/prose states, missing context and retained failures. Preserve existing
   records and receipt verification. Run appropriate full/isolated checks after the
   final code change. Tests prove contract behavior, not biological entailment.
5. **Freeze any later development experiment separately.** Keep a declared source
   frame, budget, utility/error rules and pre-output reference/prediction seals.
   No automatic retry, source substitution, answer injection or unbounded prompt
   tuning. Do not rerun this failed plan. Independent AI review must inspect every
   generated clause and resolve disagreements by evidence. New generation stays
   gated until the new design/specification and code are reviewed and frozen.
6. **Resume fresh paired feasibility only if qualification passes.** Then prepare
   fresh source-only questions/references and matched baselines/ablations. Keep
   sample dependence, exposed development cases and shared-AI limitations explicit.
   A development pass would still not qualify clinical scoring or complete T6/H5.
