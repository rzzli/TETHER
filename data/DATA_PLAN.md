## 3. Minimum scientific data contracts

### Clinical programs and outcomes

Retain stable trial/program/molecule/target/indication IDs; target action and modality; sponsor; state at origin; mapping evidence, confidence and public availability; event type/date; first-public date; source record version; last observed follow-up; stop reason; censoring/interval uncertainty; and adjudication history.

Preserve programs with unknown or immature outcomes in the at-risk frame. “Completed,” “NEVER,” an absent endpoint or no later trial is not automatically a biological failure. A higher-phase trial is a development event, not direct proof of earlier efficacy. Target-indication aggregation and program multiplicity are future protocol choices that need explicit justification.

Missing as-of target/MoA links remain missing or enter a quarantined oracle-mapping sensitivity set. Later literature or ChEMBL annotations cannot silently establish historical eligibility. Model training/scoring features never consume the evaluator's future events or gold answers.

### Genetics and LD

Require complete relevant regional tested variants, not only significant hits. Retain gene/protein/assay/isoform, tissue, phenotype definition, effect scale/unit, genome build, chromosome/position/ref/alt, effect/other allele, beta, standard error, p-value encoding, allele frequency, sample size/case-control counts as needed, ancestry, cohort membership/overlap and exact release.

LD requires compatible ancestry/build/alleles/variant ordering and reference sample selection; broad EUR is not a guaranteed match for Finnish or Icelandic studies. Record sample overlap between UKB-PPP and UKB-derived outcomes. Statistical computation must preserve weak/inconclusive/QC-failed evidence rather than relaxing thresholds to manufacture instruments.

The catalog distinguishes molecular QTL exposures from outcome GWAS. Outcome records require phenotype definition/type, effect scale/units and overlap metadata; they do not require a gene-specific assay or gene assignment. Binary phenotypes have conditional case/control counts and study case fraction; quantitative phenotypes have transformation/variance requirements when needed by the selected method. Unknown or non-applicable quantities remain explicit rather than becoming zero. A study case fraction is not population disease prevalence.

An IGSR phased genotype panel is an input to a later LD calculation, not a delivered LD matrix. Calculated results retain the reference release, method, variant order and input hashes. Study-provided LD matrices have their own orientation, matrix-type and sample-selection requirements. OpenGWAS services use endpoint-specific metadata/response contracts rather than inheriting an assumed complete QTL-file schema.

The sign of abundance MR does not automatically identify inhibition/activation. Technical binding effects, protein function versus abundance, tissue mismatch, lifelong genetics, assay platform and nonlinear dosing require separate interpretation and triangulation.

### Expression, perturbation and structure

Expression/spatial inputs retain donor, specimen, condition, assay, batch, annotation version, QC and original study identity. Cell counts are not biological replicate counts. Perturbation inputs retain guide/compound identity, cellular origin, dose/time, controls and replicates.

Structural features retain protein sequence/isoform, chain, coordinates/model version, confidence, ligand context and software parameters. A pocket score or protein abundance does not establish potency, delivery or surface accessibility.

## 6. Acquisition sequence and gates

### Stage 1 — Foundation (P0)

1. Resolve the scientific origin/horizon/model questions and identify candidate source releases. This scaffold records those unresolved choices rather than making them.
2. Restore one matched origin/follow-up AACT pair and one relevant historical Open Targets/ChEMBL bundle; inspect actual file schemas and release provenance.
3. Build versioned identifier/disease mappings and trial-to-program mapping with explicit ambiguities.
4. Construct the at-risk frame, dated events and censoring; independently audit mappings and dates. Existing weak labels can seed curation but are not automatic ground truth.
5. Document native-to-25.09 component coverage and excluded clinical/future fields. Prepare approved-source/evaluator-only manifest rules before any agent evaluation.

Gate: working artifact access, verified terms, reproducible restore, measured footprint, valid provenance and a clinically coherent cohort. A link, listing or impressive row count is insufficient.

### Stage 2 — Primary scientific extensions (P1)

1. Select historically eligible molecular exposures and endpoint-compatible outcomes; confirm complete cis statistics and matched LD.
2. Validate assay/protein/gene mapping, ancestry, allele/build harmonization, sample overlap, QC and inconclusive cases.
3. Freeze a bounded dated literature corpus; grade study design and record exact text/license/correction versions.
4. Add direction/phenome-wide and selected functional screens where coverage supports interpretation.
5. Create development-only computation/gold/canary checks before constructing feature/scorer inputs.

Gate: complete eligible inputs, meaningful positive/negative/inconclusive checks and coverage reporting. No threshold relaxation or newer replacement dataset to hide a failed data gate.

### Stage 3 — Broader assessment (P2)

Acquire only processed slices needed for expression, proteomics, pathways, structures, modality rules, component comparisons and blinded dossier audits. Use selected donors/studies; preserve normalization and biological replication. Add derived outputs and model/process provenance after actual implementations are separately authorized.

Gate: measured value/applicability and acceptable cost. Optional datasets and complete proprietary component environments do not become prerequisites.

### Stage 4 — Later extensions (P3)

Spatial/signature reversal, raw reprocessing, pharmacovigilance, patents, prospective scoring and H6 have separate data-access, cost and methodology gates. Restricted assets remain isolated or omitted; controlled patient sequencing is outside the default route.

The H6 original trial-feature matrix, exact preprocessing and model code are **unresolved**. Record whether original assets become available or any reconstruction differs. No authors are contacted by this scaffold.

## 9. Manifest and validation rules

A future artifact manifest records dataset/provider/release IDs, approved profile/origin, original unsigned source URL, retrieval timestamp, size and hash, source/annotation/method dates, schema and pipeline versions, dependency/overlap group, QC/coverage and terms/attribution. Public manifests expose only sanitized metadata.

Keep secrets, signed URLs, private paths/hosts, donor/patient identifiers, future outcomes and evaluator answers out of `manifests/public/`. The initial metadata observation record is [[data/manifests/public/2026-10-09-source-revalidation.json|the source-revalidation manifest]]. It is not a dataset manifest or successful ingestion record.

Future agent manifests allow only approved origin-eligible slices, not the full data root. Evaluator assets remain separately mounted/permissioned and network egress must be controlled. Gitignore is a version-control safeguard, not a confidentiality or time-travel mechanism.

Validation stages after later acquisition:
1. Access/rights: exact file reachable under permitted account/terms; costs bounded.
2. Integrity: complete transfer and source/hash validation; all required parts/indices present.
3. Schema: types/keys/units/row-level context inspected; minimum normalized contract satisfied.
4. Identity and coverage: joins, ambiguity, missingness and duplicates measured; no silent losses.
5. Temporal: every input/annotation has a verified basis; unknown dates excluded or explicitly reconstructed.
6. Scientific: genetics/replication/censoring/assay assumptions checked independently.
7. Evaluation isolation: source/labels/gold split; canary/access tests and complete failure accounting.

The initial implementation checked documentation/catalog integrity, source coverage, references, placeholder layout, ignore behavior and preservation of existing plans. The later authorized acquisition wave adds per-file transport/integrity receipts. Source/schema, temporal and scientific validation remain separate; use the acquisition report for actual per-product progress rather than treating the seven stages as globally complete.
