# Provenance manifests

Original acquisition and experiment manifests remain unchanged locally and in
the [historical checkpoint](https://github.com/rzzli/TETHER/tree/tether-pre-namespace-migration-20261106/data/manifests).
Their original hashes and identities are not rewritten by the namespace migration.

New products need new, explicitly bound manifests. Keep private paths, credentials,
signed links and bulk data out of published provenance records.
